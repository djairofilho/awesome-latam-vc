# Latin Caribbean venture-fund re-audit

Cutoff: 2026-07-30. Epic: #258. Execution issues: #304–#308.

## Territorial scope

- Dominican Republic (`DO`)
- Puerto Rico (`PR`)
- Cuba (`CU`)
- Haiti (`HT`)

Every geography and market-access claim requires explicit source wording. A
general Caribbean or global thesis is not silently expanded into access to all
four territories.

## Protocol

- Discovery is non-regulatory.
- Regulators may resolve a specific identity or divergence only.
- No regulator can establish eligibility.
- Direct recurring startup investment, current activity, market access and an
  official identity are separate gates.
- Accelerators, angel networks, platforms and public programs are routed.
- The local startup archive is prohibited for discovery, prioritization,
  evidence and decisions.
- Coverage is audited coverage, not a claim of market totality.

The baseline, historical delta and source plan were frozen before new candidate
records were created.

## Pre-freeze result

The independent blind search was completed without access to the candidate
list. It added 29 canonical leads; each lead now has a terminal decision and
evidence record.

- Canonical candidates: 53
- Proposed eligible additions: Venture.do and ATO Ventures
- Existing-profile corrections: Caricaco Ventures, Parallel18 Ventures, Morro
  Ventures and Innogen Capital Ventures
- Routed: 25
- Excluded: 9
- Insufficient evidence: 10
- Unchanged or routed duplicates: 7

The proposed publication set contains six actions in one batch. It remains
unfrozen until the integrator approves the reconciled review gate.

## Regulator use

Two targeted regulator queries resolved the identities of FCDS Advanced and
Semillero Investment Fund I. Neither query supplied eligibility evidence.
After the blind search expanded the universe, regulator use became 2 of 53
candidates, or 3.77%. No purposeless query was added merely to restore the
initial 5%–10% planning target.

## Territorial outcome

- Dominican Republic produced one eligible new fund, Venture.do.
- Puerto Rico produced one eligible new fund, ATO Ventures, plus two existing
  base/profile corrections.
- Cuba produced only programs, competitions, networks and public support in the
  audited sources.
- Haiti produced angel, diaspora-platform, accelerator, public-program and
  historical leads, but no manager with enough current recurring deployment
  evidence.

These are statements about audited public evidence through the cutoff date, not
claims that no other manager exists.
