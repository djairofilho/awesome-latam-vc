# Inclusion

The directory contains entities accepted into the repository's five catalog
types: venture funds, accelerators, angel networks, funding platforms and
public programs. Inclusion records documented relevance to startup funding in
Latin America; it is not an endorsement, ranking or statement of availability.

## Included entities

Every published entry has a canonical Markdown profile, structured identity,
source list and verification date. Category-specific research artifacts in the
repository document the evidence and decisions that produced accepted entries.

## Excluded entities

A temporary offer does not become a permanent entity solely because it was
open during research. A service provider, community or other adjacent actor is
not silently reclassified as a funder. Candidates without enough evidence stay
outside the published catalog until the relevant research process resolves
them.

## Decision boundaries

Entity type, geography, stage and focus come from the canonical record.
Unavailable fields remain unavailable. Inclusion does not imply that an entity
is currently accepting applications, making investments or operating every
program described in historical evidence.
