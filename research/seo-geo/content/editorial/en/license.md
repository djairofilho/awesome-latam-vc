# License and reuse

Awesome LatAm VC uses two licenses. Original catalog content, profiles,
translations, datasets, research records, and editorial work use the CC0 1.0
Universal public-domain dedication. Site code, scripts, tests, automation, and
tools use the MIT License. The repository's `LICENSE` file defines the complete
scope.

## Repository content

CC0 permits unrestricted reuse of the project's own catalog and editorial work
without requiring attribution. Reusers should inspect the legal text rather
than treat this page as legal advice.

## Software

The MIT License permits use, modification, distribution, sublicensing, and sale
of the project's software. Copies or substantial portions must retain the MIT
copyright and permission notice.

## Third-party material

Linked websites, official source titles, trademarks and organization names
remain associated with their respective owners. A link from the directory does
not relicense the destination or imply endorsement.

## Attribution

CC0 does not require attribution for the project's catalog and data. A citation
is still recommended because it lets readers locate the dataset version and
audit its sources. MIT-licensed software must retain the notice required by the
MIT License.
