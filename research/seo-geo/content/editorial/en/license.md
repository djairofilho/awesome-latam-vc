# License and reuse

The Awesome LatAm VC repository is released under the CC0 1.0 Universal public
domain dedication described in its `LICENSE` file. The license also disclaims
warranties. Source links and names in the catalog still identify material and
organizations that the project does not own.

## Repository content

The repository's license permits reuse of the project's own catalog and
editorial work under CC0. Reusers should inspect the legal text rather than
treat this page as legal advice.

## Third-party material

Linked websites, official source titles, trademarks and organization names
remain associated with their respective owners. A link from the directory does
not relicense the destination or imply endorsement.

## Attribution

CC0 does not require attribution for the project's own work. A citation is
still recommended because it lets readers locate the dataset version and audit
its sources.
