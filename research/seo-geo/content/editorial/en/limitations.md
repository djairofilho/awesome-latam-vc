# Limitations

Awesome LatAm VC is a source-backed directory, not a complete market census or
an availability service. Its entries reflect accepted repository research and
the evidence recorded on each verification date. Absence from the catalog does
not prove that an entity or funding route does not exist.

## Coverage limitations

Coverage varies by entity type, country and the public evidence available to
the project. Counts describe published profiles, not total market size,
investment volume or quality.

## Data limitations

Web sources can change or disappear. A declared thesis can differ from observed
portfolio signals. A historical program page does not prove that applications
are currently open. Translation status describes editorial review, not factual
recency.

## How to interpret absence

`null`, `not_disclosed` and `NOT_DISCLOSED` mean the canonical evidence did not
support a public value under the contract. They must not be interpreted as
zero. Missing search visibility or generative citations likewise cannot be
inferred from internal technical checks.
