# InvertUP

InvertUP is a Costa Rica-based private investment vehicle providing seed capital to a diversified portfolio of early-stage startups.

## Investment profile

- **Website:** https://invertup.com/
- **Fund type:** Private seed investment vehicle
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes
- **Open to external founders:** Yes
- **Stage at entry:** seed
- **Follow-on stages:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Focus:** sector agnostic, innovative companies
- **Geography:** CR
- **Initial check:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Investment role:** Seed investor; ParqueTec performs selection and support
- **Business models:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Portfolio size:** 7 startups on the reviewed official portfolio
- **Selected companies:** Grab & Eat, Audazzio, Ainnova Tech, Tecnología Virtual
- **Submit a startup:** https://www.parquetec.org/general-7

## Declared thesis

InvertUP invests small seed-capital amounts in innovative early-stage companies. ParqueTec supplies the pipeline, due diligence, mentoring and founder intake.

## Portfolio signals

The current official portfolio names seven startup investments and states that new companies will be added continuously.
