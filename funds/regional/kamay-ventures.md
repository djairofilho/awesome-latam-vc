# Kamay Ventures

Kamay Ventures is an Argentina-based multi-corporate venture capital fund investing in startups that transform the consumer-goods value chain in Latin America.

## Investment profile

- **Website:** https://kamayventures.com/
- **Fund type:** Corporate venture capital
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes
- **Open to external founders:** Yes
- **Stage at entry:** Seed and Series A
- **Follow-on stages:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Focus:** Technology for the consumer-goods value chain
- **Geography:** Latin America
- **Initial check:** Up to US$600,000
- **Investment role:** Co-lead or follow a strong lead
- **Business models:** Artificial intelligence, finance, data, supply-chain efficiency, logistics, agriculture, and energy
- **Portfolio size:** 22 companies, as reported on the official website
- **Selected companies:** AltScore, Kilimo, Sensify, Wisy, and DeepAgro
- **Submit a startup:** https://kamayventures.com/

## Declared thesis

Kamay Ventures describes itself as Latin America's first multi-corporate venture fund. It invests up to US$600,000 at Seed and pre-Series A, co-leading or following a strong lead, and connects startups with its corporate partners.

## Portfolio signals

The official portfolio includes financial infrastructure, agriculture, retail execution, industrial sensing, and logistics companies, consistent with the fund's consumer-goods value-chain thesis.
