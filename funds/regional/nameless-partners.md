# Nameless Partners

Nameless Partners is a Latin American venture capital firm built in Brazil
that invests directly at the earliest stages.

## Investment profile

- **Website:** https://www.nameless.partners/
- **Fund type:** Venture capital
- **Operator:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes
- **Open to external founders:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Stage at entry:** Earliest stages; a normalized stage is not publicly disclosed
- **Follow-on stages:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Focus:** Founders; a sector mandate is not publicly disclosed
- **Geography:** Latin America
- **Initial check:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Investment role:** Early backer; lead or co-investor preference is not publicly disclosed
- **Business models:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Portfolio size:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Selected companies:** Coffex, Cafello, and Core AI
- **Submit a startup:** Not publicly disclosed

## Declared thesis

Nameless Partners says it invests before a founder has a finished pitch,
product, or even a named thesis. It emphasizes resilient and ambitious founders,
active strategic support, and long-term partnership. The reviewed official
sources do not disclose a sector mandate, normalized stage, check size, or
founder submission route.

## Portfolio signals

The official LinkedIn profile confirms an investment in Coffex and identifies
the founder of Cafello as a portfolio founder. Norte Ventures also named
Nameless Partners as an investor in the Core AI round. These activity signals
confirm direct and recent startup investment but are not used to infer a broader
sector mandate.
