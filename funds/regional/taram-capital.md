# Taram Capital

Taram Capital is a Chile-based venture capital fund investing in B2B and AI-first SaaS startups across Latin America.

## Investment profile

- **Website:** https://www.taramcapital.com/
- **Fund type:** Venture capital
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes
- **Open to external founders:** Yes
- **Stage at entry:** Seed and Series A
- **Follow-on stages:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Focus:** AI-first B2B software and infrastructure for traditional industries
- **Geography:** Latin America, with emphasis on companies operating in Chile
- **Initial check:** US$1 million to US$3 million; US$100,000 to US$500,000 for earlier-stage “Pay to See” investments
- **Investment role:** Lead and co-lead
- **Business models:** B2B and SaaS
- **Portfolio size:** Not publicly disclosed in the reviewed source
- **Selected companies:** HealthAtom, Webdox, and SimpliRoute
- **Submit a startup:** [Founder application](https://www.taramcapital.com/)

## Declared thesis

Taram Capital leads and co-leads Late Seed and Series A rounds in Latin American B2B software and infrastructure companies using AI to automate critical work in traditional industries. It also makes smaller “Pay to See” investments in promising earlier-stage companies.

## Portfolio signals

The selected official portfolio examples serve healthcare, contract management, and last-mile logistics. They align with the declared focus on AI-enabled software for operationally intensive industries.
