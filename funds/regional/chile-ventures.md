# Chile Ventures

Chile Ventures is a Chile-based venture capital firm that invests in recurring-revenue technology startups serving Latin America.

## Investment profile

- **Website:** https://chileventures.vc/en
- **Fund type:** Venture capital
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes
- **Open to external founders:** Yes
- **Stage at entry:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Follow-on stages:** Yes; the fund states that it invests more when companies progress as expected
- **Focus:** Technology startups with recurring revenue through SaaS, subscriptions, or licenses
- **Geography:** Chile and Latin America
- **Initial check:** US$50,000 to US$1 million
- **Investment role:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Business models:** SaaS, subscriptions, and licensing
- **Portfolio size:** Three companies shown on the reviewed page
- **Selected companies:** Healthatom, Vambe, and Shinkansen
- **Submit a startup:** [Send a deck](https://chileventures.vc/en)

## Declared thesis

Chile Ventures invests between US$50,000 and US$1 million in technology startups with recurring revenue. Its published process covers scouting, investment analysis, committee review, due diligence, disbursement, board participation, and follow-on support.

## Portfolio signals

The three companies highlighted on the official page provide healthcare software, AI-based sales automation, and financial infrastructure for Latin America. These examples are observations, not an expansion of the fund's declared recurring-revenue thesis.
