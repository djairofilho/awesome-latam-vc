# Impacta VC

Impacta VC is a Chile-based venture capital firm backing impact-driven founders across Latin America.

## Investment profile

- **Website:** https://www.impacta.vc/
- **Fund type:** Venture capital
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes
- **Open to external founders:** Yes
- **Stage at entry:** Seed
- **Follow-on stages:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Focus:** Social and environmental impact
- **Geography:** Latin America
- **Initial check:** US$100,000 to US$500,000; the site states a US$300,000 average Seed investment
- **Investment role:** Lead and co-lead
- **Business models:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Portfolio size:** More than seven startups; target portfolio of 20
- **Selected companies:** Betterfly, Wheel the World, Kilimo, Lab4U, and Cuéntame
- **Submit a startup:** [Apply to Impacta VC](https://www.impacta.vc/)

## Declared thesis

Impacta VC invests in Latin American founders whose companies combine business growth with measurable social or environmental impact. The fund leads and co-leads Seed rounds and reserves capital for follow-on investments.

## Portfolio signals

The official examples span insurtech, accessible travel, agricultural water management, science education, and mental health. These observations illustrate the impact thesis but do not define additional formal sector restrictions.
