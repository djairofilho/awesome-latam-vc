# 500 LatAm (500 Global)

500 LatAm is 500 Global's early-stage strategy for technology companies founded
in Spanish-speaking Latin America.

## Investment profile

- **Website:** https://500.co/latam
- **Fund type:** Venture capital
- **Stage at entry:** Pre-seed and Seed
- **Follow-on stages:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Focus:** Technology, sector agnostic
- **Geography:** Spanish-speaking Latin America
- **Initial check:** USD 300,000 under the current accelerator offer
- **Investment role:** Direct investor through the 500 LatAm Accelerator
- **Business models:** Software, applications, no-code tools, and other
  technology-enabled scalable models
- **Portfolio size:** More than 300 regionally headquartered startups, according
  to 500 Global
- **Selected companies:** 99 Minutos, Conekta, Jüsto, and Platzi
- **Submit a startup:** https://latam.aplica.500.co/en/sites/latam

## Declared thesis

500 LatAm backs early-stage Latin American founders building technology
companies with the potential to scale beyond the region. Its current accelerator
offer is open to companies across sectors and does not require initial revenue.

## Portfolio signals

The local project dataset contains 165 companies associated with this fund. Its
most frequent categories include fintech, education, e-commerce, SME solutions,
and HR tech. These observations describe the collected portfolio and are not the
fund's declared sector mandate.
