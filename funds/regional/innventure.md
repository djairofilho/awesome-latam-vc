# Innventure

Innventure is an Argentina-based venture capital fund specializing in Latin American AgriFoodTech.

## Investment profile

- **Website:** https://innventure.com.ar/
- **Fund type:** Venture capital
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes
- **Open to external founders:** Yes
- **Stage at entry:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Follow-on stages:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Focus:** AgTech and AgriFoodTech
- **Geography:** Latin America
- **Initial check:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Investment role:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Business models:** Technology for producing, processing, and distributing food with economic, social, and environmental impact
- **Portfolio size:** More than 18 invested startups, as reported on the official website
- **Selected companies:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Submit a startup:** https://innventure.com.ar/

## Declared thesis

Innventure states that it invests in Latin American startups transforming how food is produced, processed, and distributed. Its public site reports more than 18 invested startups and provides an external application channel.

The official site reports an AgriFoodTech portfolio with more than 18 investments, but the reviewed page does not expose a reliable text list of representative companies.
