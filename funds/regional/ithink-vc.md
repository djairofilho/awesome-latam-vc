# iThink VC

iThink VC invests in technology companies from emerging Latin American
ecosystems.

## Investment profile

- **Website:** https://www.ithink.vc/
- **Fund type:** Venture capital
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes
- **Open to external founders:** Yes
- **Stage at entry:** Pre-seed and Seed
- **Follow-on stages:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Focus:** Technology solving structural problems in Latin America
- **Geography:** Paraguay, Peru, Ecuador, Bolivia, and Uruguay
- **Initial check:** USD 100,000 to USD 500,000
- **Investment role:** Early-stage investor
- **Business models:** Technology-enabled companies
- **Portfolio size:** 20 investments
- **Selected companies:** Storybook, Xeptiva, Reforest Latam, and Mobi
- **Submit a startup:** https://www.ithink.vc/

## Declared thesis

The fund is sector agnostic and focuses on technology companies addressing
structural problems in emerging Latin American markets. It operates from
Asunción and publicly invites founders raising capital.
