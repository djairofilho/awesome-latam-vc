SF500 is an Argentina-based life sciences fund and company-building program that turns scientific projects into globally scalable startups.

- **Website:** https://sf500.vc/
- **Fund type:** Venture builder and venture capital
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes
- **Open to external founders:** Yes
- **Stage at entry:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Follow-on stages:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Focus:** Science-based startups and biotechnology
- **Geography:** Argentina and Latin America

- **Investment role:** First investor
- **Business models:** Science-based companies; further constraints are not publicly disclosed
- **Portfolio size:** Not publicly disclosed in the reviewed source
- **Selected companies:** Not publicly disclosed in the reviewed source

## Declared thesis

SF500 identifies scientific and business talent, builds science-based startups, and invests US$200,000 for a 20% stake as the company's first investor. Its SFBuild program is temporarily closed, but the fund continues to accept contact details for future editions.
