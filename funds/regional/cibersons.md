# Cibersons

Cibersons is a Paraguay-based technology investment group backing early-stage
startups in Latin America and the United States.

## Investment profile

- **Website:** https://www.cibersons.com/
- **Fund type:** Venture capital and technology investment group
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes
- **Open to external founders:** Yes
- **Stage at entry:** Pre-seed, Seed, and Series A
- **Focus:** Technology, sector agnostic
- **Geography:** Latin America and the United States
- **Initial check:** Up to USD 500,000 in the reviewed industry directory
- **Portfolio size:** 39 investments across 13 countries in the reviewed directory
- **Selected companies:** CargoX, DeltaX, Nuvocargo, Kiwibot, Tractian, Kushki, Terapify, and Global 66
- **Submit a startup:** https://www.cibersons.com/

## Declared thesis

The manager invests in technology companies and other funds. Its official
ventures page separates fund and startup investments and displays a broad
technology portfolio.

## Base geography

Paraguay is recorded as the strict base. LAVCA states that the investor is based
in Asunción, the Paraguayan Ministry of Industry and Commerce lists Cibersons
Paraguay at an Asunción address, and URUCAP reports headquarters in Paraguay and
Silicon Valley. This classification is not inferred from founder nationality or
association membership.
