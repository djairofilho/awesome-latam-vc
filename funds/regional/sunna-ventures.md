# Sunna Ventures

Sunna Ventures is a Chile-based early-stage climate technology venture capital firm connecting Latin America with the global innovation ecosystem.

## Investment profile

- **Website:** https://sunna.vc/
- **Fund type:** Venture capital
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes
- **Open to external founders:** Yes
- **Stage at entry:** Pre-seed, Seed, and Series A
- **Follow-on stages:** Series A
- **Focus:** Climate technology, industrial decarbonization, critical minerals, energy, and circularity
- **Geography:** Latin America and North America
- **Initial check:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Investment role:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Business models:** Climate technology across industrial and financial systems
- **Portfolio size:** 13 companies listed on the official portfolio page
- **Selected companies:** Andes, Backchannel, Bia, Solara, and Unblock Computing
- **Submit a startup:** [Join the portfolio](https://sunna.vc/)

## Declared thesis

Sunna invests from company formation through Series A in teams advancing the energy transition and industrial decarbonization across Latin America and North America. Its stated areas include critical minerals, energy, circularity, and industrial climate solutions.

## Portfolio signals

The official portfolio includes companies based in Brazil, Colombia, Mexico, Argentina, Chile, the United States, Switzerland, and the United Kingdom. This geographic mix supports the declared bridge between Latin American and global climate innovation.
