# Yango Ventures

Yango Ventures is a Dubai-based corporate venture fund investing from Seed to
Series B across emerging markets, including Latin America.

## Investment profile

- **Website:** https://ventures.yango.com/
- **Fund type:** Corporate venture capital
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes
- **Open to external founders:** Yes
- **Stage at entry:** Seed to Series B, with Series A as the preferred stage
- **Focus:** Online-to-offline, Fintech, B2B SaaS, and artificial intelligence
- **Geography:** Latin America and other emerging markets; explicit access in Bolivia
- **Fund size:** USD 20 million at launch
- **Investment role:** Follow-on investor alongside a lead
- **Submit a startup:** https://ventures.yango.com/contact-us

## Declared thesis

The fund invests Yango Group capital in emerging-market technology companies.
Its current site states a Seed-to-Series-B mandate and invites founders to send
a deck. An April 2025 launch in Bolivia establishes local access; it does not
make the fund Bolivia-based.

## Portfolio signals

The official portfolio includes companies in Fintech, logistics, e-commerce and
business automation across Africa, MENA, Pakistan and Central Asia. The fund was
designed to scale beyond its initial USD 20 million.
