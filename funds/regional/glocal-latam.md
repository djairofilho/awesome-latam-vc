# GLOCAL Latam

GLOCAL Latam is an Argentina-based accelerator and venture capital fund investing in Latin American AgriFoodTech and ClimateTech startups.

GLOCAL combines acceleration with direct investment in early-stage AgriFoodTech and ClimateTech startups across Latin America. It discloses financing of up to US$500,000 and operates the Game Changers application program.
