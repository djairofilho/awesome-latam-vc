# Salkantay Ventures

Salkantay Ventures is an early-stage fund backing technology companies that
address social, economic, and environmental challenges in Latin America.

## Investment profile

- **Website:** https://www.salkantay.vc/
- **Fund type:** Venture capital
- **Stage at entry:** Pre-seed, Seed, and Series A
- **Follow-on stages:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Focus:** Impact technology
- **Geography:** Spanish-speaking Latin America
- **Initial check:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Investment role:** Strategic partner; lead or co-investment preference is
  not publicly disclosed
- **Business models:** Technology-enabled businesses, including SaaS, fintech,
  proptech, edtech, healthtech, agtech, and climate tech
- **Portfolio size:** 23 companies
- **Selected companies:** Aprende Institute, Cometa, Equip, Manzana Verde, and
  Mercately
- **Submit a startup:** https://www.salkantay.vc/contact

## Declared thesis

Salkantay invests from Pre-seed through Series A in Latin American founders
serving Spanish-speaking markets. Its thesis covers education, jobs, health,
financial inclusion, SME digitization, climate, agriculture, and smart cities.
