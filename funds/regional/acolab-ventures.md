# Açolab Ventures

Açolab Ventures is ArcelorMittal Brasil's corporate venture capital vehicle for
startups that can strengthen the steel value chain.

## Investment profile

- **Website:** https://qas-brasil.arcelormittal.com.br/sustentabilidade-inovacao/inovacao/acolab
- **Fund type:** Corporate venture capital vehicle
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes
- **Open to external founders:** Yes
- **Stage at entry:** Validated product and at least BRL 30,000 in monthly revenue
- **Follow-on stages:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Focus:** Steel, construction, industrial productivity, logistics, digitalization, and sustainability
- **Geography:** Latin America
- **Initial check:** BRL 1 million to BRL 15 million
- **Investment role:** Strategic investor, with co-investment possible
- **Business models:** B2B, B2B2C, and B2C
- **Portfolio size:** 2 startups displayed on the official page
- **Selected companies:** Agilean and Sirros IoT
- **Submit a startup:** Not publicly disclosed for a current application channel

## Declared thesis

The vehicle invests in operating Latin American startups with validated
products and direct strategic fit with ArcelorMittal's steel value chain. Its
themes include construction, customer experience, alternative materials,
logistics, productivity, cost reduction, environmental efficiency, sales
channels, and industrial digitalization.

Açolab Ventures is the investment vehicle within Açolab, ArcelorMittal
Brasil's broader open-innovation hub. Proofs of concept, challenges, and pitch
days run by the hub are not treated as investments unless the official source
also identifies an investment.

## Portfolio signals

The official page displays Agilean and Sirros IoT and describes technical
support, governance, operational synergies, infrastructure, and customer access
as part of its smart-money model. The displayed total is used as the public
portfolio count without inferring additional investments from Açolab's
innovation projects.
