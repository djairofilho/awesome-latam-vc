# IMPAQTO Capital

IMPAQTO Capital provides flexible impact financing to early-stage companies in
the Andean region.

## Investment profile

- **Website:** https://www.impaqtocapital.com/es
- **Fund type:** Impact investment fund
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes
- **Open to external founders:** Yes
- **Stage at entry:** Early-stage companies; named rounds are not publicly disclosed
- **Follow-on stages:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Focus:** Social and environmental impact with a gender lens
- **Geography:** Ecuador, Peru, Bolivia, Colombia, and Chile
- **Initial check:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Investment role:** Flexible revenue-based financing, primarily through non-dilutive loans
- **Business models:** Impact startups and SMEs with annual revenue from USD 200,000 to USD 3 million
- **Portfolio size:** 12 investments
- **Selected companies:** ELSA, Fibrazo, Sinba, Cusi, Quenco, and tuGerente
- **Submit a startup:** https://airtable.com/app8iMZm0TA0zE1Dk/shrGNR0y7cKK9R9Gg

## Declared thesis

The Quito-based manager designs and operates its own impact funds for companies
with measurable social or environmental outcomes in the Andean region. Its
current criteria include operations in Ecuador, Peru, Bolivia, Colombia, or
Chile and a regional, scalable business model.

## Portfolio signals

The official site reports 12 investments across 4 countries. In January 2025,
IMPAQTO Capital announced the USD 2.1 million final close of Fund I, which had
already invested in 6 companies headquartered across Ecuador, Peru, Colombia,
and Bolivia.
