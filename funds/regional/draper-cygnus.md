# Draper Cygnus

Draper Cygnus is an Argentina-based venture capital firm investing in early-stage deep-tech companies founded by Latin American entrepreneurs.

## Investment profile

- **Website:** https://www.drapercygnus.vc/
- **Fund type:** Venture capital
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes
- **Open to external founders:** Yes
- **Stage at entry:** Seed and Series A
- **Follow-on stages:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Focus:** Deep tech
- **Geography:** Latin American founders building globally
- **Initial check:** US$100,000 to US$1 million
- **Investment role:** Lead and co-investor
- **Business models:** Artificial intelligence, blockchain, synthetic biology, space, and cybersecurity
- **Portfolio size:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Selected companies:** Novo Space, Max Space, Skyloom, Splight, and Eywa Biotech
- **Submit a startup:** https://www.drapercygnus.vc/contact

## Declared thesis

Draper Cygnus states that it backs deep-tech companies as early as possible and invests US$100,000 to US$1 million. Its team describes leading Seed and Series A investments for Latin American founders pursuing global markets.

## Portfolio signals

The official portfolio includes space infrastructure, biotechnology, energy systems, and communications companies, consistent with the firm's declared deep-tech mandate.
