# Caricaco Ventures

Caricaco Ventures invests in software and AI-native startups connected to
Central America and the Dominican Republic.

## Investment profile

- **Website:** https://caricaco.vc/
- **Fund type:** Venture capital
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes
- **Open to external founders:** Yes
- **Stage at entry:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Follow-on stages:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Focus:** Software, artificial intelligence, and purpose-driven technology
- **Geography:** Central America and the Dominican Republic
- **Initial check:** From USD 200,000
- **Investment role:** First-check investor
- **Business models:** Software and AI-native startups with an MVP
- **Portfolio size:** More than 27 startups
- **Selected companies:** Vitrinnea, TobiPets, Snap Compliance, and Zunify
- **Submit a startup:** https://caricaco.vc/aplicar/

## Declared thesis

CV2 backs founders from Central America and the Dominican Republic, wherever
they build, as well as founders with headquarters or meaningful operations in
the region. Caricaco became an active investor in 2021 after beginning as an
academy and incubator, so community programs are kept separate from investment.

## Portfolio signals

The official 2026 launch reports 27 companies across six countries and plans
15 to 16 new investments through CV2.
