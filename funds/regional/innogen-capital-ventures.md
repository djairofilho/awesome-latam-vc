# Innogen Capital Ventures

Innogen Capital Ventures invests in early-stage technology companies across
northern Latin America.

## Investment profile

- **Website:** https://innogencapital.com/
- **Fund type:** Venture capital
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes
- **Open to external founders:** Yes
- **Stage at entry:** Pre-seed, Seed, and Series A
- **Follow-on stages:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Focus:** Financial inclusion, healthcare, retail, logistics, proptech, and agtech
- **Geography:** Mexico, Colombia, Central America, and the Caribbean
- **Initial check:** USD 25,000 to USD 1 million
- **Investment role:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Business models:** High-impact technology companies
- **Portfolio size:** 28 investments deployed
- **Selected companies:** Boxful, Ginih, and Instaleap
- **Submit a startup:** https://innogencapital.com/en/

## Declared thesis

Innogen invests in transformative technologies with exponential scaling
potential. Delta Fund targets Guatemala, Honduras, and El Salvador, while Fund
II targets Mexico and Colombia with opportunistic investments elsewhere in
Latin America.

## Portfolio signals

The official site reports 9 Fund I investments, 13 Fund II investments, and 6
Delta investments. Boxful and Ginih confirm current exposure to El Salvador and
Honduras.
