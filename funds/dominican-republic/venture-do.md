# Venture.do

Venture.do is a Dominican Republic-based early-stage fund investing in B2B
software startups across Latin America.

## Investment profile

- **Website:** https://venture.do/
- **Fund type:** Venture capital
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes
- **Open to external founders:** Yes
- **Stage at entry:** Pre-seed and Seed
- **Follow-on stages:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Focus:** B2B software and SaaS
- **Geography:** Latin America
- **Initial check:** Average of USD 100,000
- **Investment role:** Early-stage direct investor
- **Business models:** Capital-efficient B2B software
- **Portfolio size:** 10 investments through Fund Zero
- **Selected companies:** AlterEstate, Avify, Logra, Ruedata, Quix, Picker, Pricelab, and Nippy
- **Submit a startup:** https://venture.do/funds

## Declared thesis

Fund Zero launched in 2022 with USD 2 million to back Pre-seed and Seed
companies across Latin America. Venture.do says it evaluated more than 200
companies for the vehicle and plans Fund One for 2026.

## Portfolio signals

The current portfolio names ten companies operating across the Dominican
Republic, Mexico, Costa Rica, Colombia, Ecuador, Peru and other Latin American
markets.
