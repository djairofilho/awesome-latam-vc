# GRPP Ventures

GRPP Ventures is Grupo RPP's corporate venture capital arm. It invests media
inventory in exchange for equity in technology-enabled companies that can
benefit from Grupo RPP's reach in Peru.

## Investment profile

- **Website:** https://grppventures.pe/
- **Fund type:** Corporate venture capital
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes
- **Open to external founders:** Yes
- **Stage at entry:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Follow-on stages:** Not publicly disclosed in the reviewed sources
- **Focus:** Scalable technology
- **Geography:** Peru
- **Initial check:** Media coverage package; monetary value not publicly disclosed
- **Investment role:** Media-for-equity investor
- **Business models:** Technology-enabled companies; specific B2B/B2C constraints not publicly disclosed
- **Portfolio size:** 14 companies displayed on the official website
- **Selected companies:** Cirkula, Emprende con Eme, Flexy Logistics, Goflex, Holos
- **Submit a startup:** [Application form](https://forms.gle/WUpqnhXSrQ6kaLLe9)

## Declared thesis

GRPP Ventures seeks founders with strategic or social alignment, validated
products, proven traction, scalable growth, experienced teams, and potential
synergy with Grupo RPP's audiences. Its official model exchanges a package of
advertising coverage for startup equity.
