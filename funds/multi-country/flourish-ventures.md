# Flourish Ventures

Flourish Ventures is a global early-stage evergreen venture fund backing
technology-enabled companies that improve financial health and prosperity.

## Investment profile

- **Website:** https://flourishventures.com/
- **Fund type:** Evergreen venture capital
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes
- **Open to external founders:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Stage at entry:** Early stage; specific entry stages are not publicly
  disclosed in the reviewed thesis
- **Follow-on stages:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Focus:** Financial health and a fair, customer-centric financial system
- **Geography:** Global, including Latin America
- **Initial check:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Investment role:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Business models:** Technology-enabled financial services; no narrower
  B2B/B2C constraint is publicly disclosed
- **Portfolio size:** 13 Latin American portfolio entries displayed on the
  official regional page, including exited companies, as of 2026-07-30
- **Selected companies:** Akua, Albo, Heru, Kamino, and Neon
- **Submit a startup:** Not publicly disclosed

## Declared thesis

Flourish Ventures makes thesis-driven venture investments in early-stage
entrepreneurs whose companies seek to improve financial health and prosperity.
The evergreen fund backs ideas that can scale and use technology responsibly
to build a more customer-centric financial sector. Its official geography
includes emerging markets, the United States, and a dedicated Latin America
investment team and portfolio.

## Portfolio signals

The official Latin America page displayed 13 portfolio entries on 2026-07-30
across digital banking, embedded finance, and financial infrastructure. This
regional count includes companies marked as exited and should not be treated as
the fund's total active portfolio size or as a substitute for its declared
thesis.
