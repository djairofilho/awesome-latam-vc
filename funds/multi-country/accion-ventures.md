# Accion Ventures

Accion Ventures is an Accion Impact Management early-stage investment strategy
with recurring startup investments and verified access to founders in Brazil.

## Declared thesis

Accion Ventures, previously known as Accion Venture Lab, is operated through
Accion Impact Management. The official strategy page describes recurring
early-stage startup investment, presents more than 80 investments, and provides
an open pitch route. The frozen evidence does not normalize its early-stage
scope into specific financing rounds.

## Portfolio signals

The strategy page presents four Brazilian companies. Accion also confirmed
that Accion Ventures participated in BackChannel's US$4.75 million seed round
on March 23, 2026. That round is an activity observation, not a general stage
claim.
