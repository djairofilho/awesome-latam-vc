# GRIDX

GRIDX is a company-building venture fund that turns Latin American bioscience
research into startups targeting global markets.

- **Website:** https://www.gridexponential.com/
- **Fund type:** Venture builder and venture capital
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes
- **Open to external founders:** Yes
- **Stage at entry:** Pre-seed
- **Follow-on stages:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Focus:** Life sciences & biotech
- **Geography:** Latin America to global markets
- **Initial check:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Investment role:** Hands-on company builder and initial investor
- **Business models:** Science-based and deep-tech ventures
- **Portfolio size:** More than 90 startups founded
- **Selected companies:** Beeflow, CASPR Biotech, Michroma, Puna Bio, and Stämm
- **Apply as a scientist:** https://www.gridexponential.com/apply-as-a-scientist
- **Apply as an entrepreneur:** https://www.gridexponential.com/apply-as-an-entrepreneur

## Declared thesis

GRIDX focuses on the bioeconomy and combines biology, digital tools, and
engineering. It works at the earliest company-formation stages, matching
scientists and business founders before preparing the resulting startups for
Seed fundraising.
