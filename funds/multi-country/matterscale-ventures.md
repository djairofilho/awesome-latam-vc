# MatterScale Ventures

MatterScale Ventures invests in Latin founders across the Americas.

## Investment profile

- **Website:** https://matterscale.com/
- **Fund type:** Venture capital
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes
- **Open to external founders:** Yes
- **Stage at entry:** Pre-seed, Seed, and Series A
- **Follow-on stages:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Focus:** Affordable and accessible technology-enabled services
- **Geography:** The Americas, including Latin America
- **Initial check:** USD 150,000 to USD 750,000
- **Investment role:** Direct investor and syndicate builder
- **Business models:** Technology-enabled services
- **Portfolio size:** 13 companies displayed on the official page
- **Selected companies:** Nilo Labs, Celes, Bia Energy, Hunty, and Manzana Verde
- **Submit a startup:** https://matterscale.com/

## Declared thesis

The fund backs Latin startups in the United States and Latin America that use
technology to democratize education, healthcare, financial services, security,
employment, and other essential services.

## Portfolio signals

The official page displays 13 companies and states that the team has local
presence across the United States, Mexico, Colombia, Ecuador, Argentina, Chile,
and Brazil.
