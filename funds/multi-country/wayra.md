# Wayra

Wayra is Telefónica's corporate venture capital and open-innovation platform,
investing in startups that can generate strategic business with the group.

## Investment profile

- **Website:** https://wayra.com/
- **Fund type:** Corporate venture capital
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes
- **Open to external founders:** Yes
- **Stage at entry:** Multi-stage
- **Follow-on stages:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Focus:** Strategic technology
- **Geography:** Brazil, Europe, and Latin America
- **Initial check:** EUR 150,000 to EUR 5 million
- **Investment role:** Minority strategic investor and co-investor in qualified rounds
- **Business models:** B2B and B2C technology models that can integrate with
  Telefónica businesses
- **Portfolio size:** Not reliably displayed on the current general website
- **Selected companies in the local dataset:** Agrolend, Conexa Saúde,
  CRM&Bonus, Docket, and Monkey
- **Submit a startup:** https://wayra.com/investment

## Declared thesis

Wayra invests in Seed and Growth companies with the potential to generate
business with Telefónica and pre-money valuations of up to EUR 250 million.
Its stated ticket range is EUR 150,000 to EUR 5 million, generally alongside
leading venture capital firms.
Specific vehicles, such as Vivo Ventures and Íope Ventures, have their own
sector, stage, and ticket rules.

## Portfolio signals

The local project dataset contains 21 companies associated with Wayra in Latin
America. The dataset mixes multiple Wayra vehicles, so its sectors and stages
must not be treated as a single official mandate.
