## Declared thesis

Antler's Brazil page documents a permanent local operation, direct investment
terms, a founder application route, and an office and team in the country. The
reviewed frozen evidence does not support a normalized sector focus, entry
stage, or initial check for this profile.

## Portfolio signals

Antler reports more than 30 companies in its Brazil portfolio. On June 16,
2026, BNDES announced an investment of up to BRL 63 million in Antler Brasil I,
a pre-seed vehicle intended to support as many as 100 Brazilian startups. The
vehicle-level pre-seed mandate is not presented as Antler's universal stage.
