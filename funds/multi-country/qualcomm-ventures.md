# Qualcomm Ventures

Qualcomm Ventures is the corporate venture capital arm of Qualcomm, with an active investment mandate and portfolio in Latin America.

## Investment profile

- **Website:** https://www.qualcommventures.com/
- **Fund type:** Corporate venture capital
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes
- **Open to external founders:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Stage at entry:** Multi-stage
- **Follow-on stages:** Growth
- **Focus:** AI, automotive, mobile, enterprise and data center, and smart systems
- **Geography:** Global, including Latin America
- **Initial check:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Investment role:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Business models:** Technology companies across software, hardware, infrastructure, and connected systems
- **Portfolio size:** Ten active Latin American portfolio companies reported in July 2024; more than 500 companies globally
- **Selected companies:** Aravita; further active Latin American companies are not named in the reviewed regional source
- **Submit a startup:** Not publicly disclosed

## Declared thesis

Qualcomm Ventures invests from early to growth stage in technology companies across AI, automotive, mobile, enterprise infrastructure, and smart systems. Its Latin America investment director reported an active regional portfolio and continued search for opportunities aligned with those areas.

## Portfolio signals

The regional source reports ten active Latin American companies and identifies Aravita as a recent investment. The global site also lists 99, a Brazilian mobility company acquired in 2018, as a historical portfolio outcome.
