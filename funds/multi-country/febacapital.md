startups.

## Declared thesis

FEBACAPITAL describes itself as a strategic venture capital firm that partners
with founders in the early stages and continues supporting them as they scale.
It combines financial capital with operating support in product, technology,
marketing, funding, and customer acquisition.

The official portfolio includes Brazilian companies across fintech, real
estate technology, human resources, education, and business software. The
firm's contact page lists offices in Boston and the Cayman Islands, so the
profile is classified as global with a Latin American presence rather than as
a Brazil-based fund.
