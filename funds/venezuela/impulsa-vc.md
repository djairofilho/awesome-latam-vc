# Impulsa VC

Impulsa VC provides early institutional capital and financial structuring to
technology companies in Venezuela.

## Investment profile

- **Website:** https://www.impulsa.vc/
- **Fund type:** Open-capital investment company
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes
- **Open to external founders:** Yes
- **Stage at entry:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Focus:** Technology and financial structuring
- **Geography:** Venezuela
- **Initial check:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Selected companies:** The current site displays portfolio companies; its recent public activity includes Duwu
- **Submit a startup:** https://www.impulsa.vc/contacto/

## Declared thesis

Impulsa describes itself as Venezuela's first institutional capital and
early-stage financial structurer. It invests in startups with potential, then
supports their financial and corporate structure so they can scale and access
additional capital. Its legal operator is Impulsa Venture Capital, C.A.

## Portfolio signals

The current site presents companies supported by the manager, and a recent
investment announcement records Duwu as a direct addition to the portfolio.
The official contact page locates the company in Caracas.
