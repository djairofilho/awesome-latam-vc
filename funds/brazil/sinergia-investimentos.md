# Sinergia Investimentos

Sinergia Investimentos is a corporate venture capital program using
adaptable capital and strategic support for startups in the Amazon bioeconomy.

## Investment profile

- **Website:** https://sinergiainvestimentos.jornadaamazonia.org.br/
- **Fund type:** Corporate venture capital program
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes
- **Open to external founders:** Yes, through periodic program batches
- **Stage at entry:** Pre-seed and Seed
- **Follow-on stages:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Focus:** Amazon bioeconomy and regenerative businesses
- **Geography:** Brazilian Amazon
- **Initial check:** BRL 300,000 to BRL 1 million
- **Investment role:** Equity investor for 3% to 15%
- **Business models:** B2B and B2C businesses using biodiversity, plus platforms, marketplaces, SaaS, and equipment
- **Portfolio size:** 20 startups displayed across Batch 1 and Batch 2
- **Selected companies:** Amazon Artesanal, ForestiFi, Genera, AmaSKN, and AeroRiver
- **Submit a startup:** Not publicly disclosed for a current investment batch

## Declared thesis

The program invests in Pre-seed and Seed startups that use biodiversity as raw
material or build cross-cutting technologies for food and beverages,
cosmetics, fine chemicals, and the Amazon bioeconomy. Its Capital Evolutivo
model combines equity with mentoring, boards, specialist access, and market
connections.

Sinergia Investimentos is integrated into Jornada Amazônia. Its Batch 3
regulation identifies Fundação CERTI, Darwin Startups, Amazônia B, and
CVentures as executors, with CVentures managing investments. Corporate and
institutional partners supplied investment capital but are not presented as
the legal vehicle. The current legal vehicle is not publicly disclosed.

## Portfolio signals

The official portfolio page displays 20 selected startups across two batches.
A separate Sinergia 2026 support cycle is coordinated by Fundação CERTI and
Instituto CERTI Amazônia and runs through May 2027. Its regulation does not
guarantee an investment, so participation in that newer support cycle is not
counted as a Sinergia Investimentos portfolio company.
