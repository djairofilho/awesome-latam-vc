# Raio Capital

Raio Capital is a Brazilian venture capital firm investing in early-stage
technology companies that improve fundamental value chains.

- **Direct startup investment:** Yes

## Declared thesis

Raio invests in early-stage technology-enabled companies that make fundamental
value chains more efficient. It looks for businesses that remove friction,
use competitive advantages to generate disproportionate returns, and improve
customer satisfaction.
