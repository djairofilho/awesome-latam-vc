# AMAZ

AMAZ is an impact accelerator and investor dedicated to businesses that create
positive outcomes for the Amazon forest and its communities.

## Investment profile

- **Website:** https://amaz.org.br/
- **Fund type:** Accelerator and venture capital
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes
- **Open to external founders:** Yes, through periodic public calls
- **Stage at entry:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Follow-on stages:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Focus:** Amazon bioeconomy, forest conservation, climate, and local income generation
- **Geography:** Brazilian Amazon
- **Initial check:** Up to BRL 1 million in the 2025 public call
- **Investment role:** Seed investor and accelerator
- **Business models:** Impact businesses serving the forest and Amazon communities
- **Portfolio size:** 21 impact businesses reported in May 2025
- **Selected companies:** AmidoMato, ForestiFi, Tribo Super Foods, Zeno Nativo, and MOMA
- **Submit a startup:** Not publicly disclosed for a current call

## Declared thesis

AMAZ accelerates and invests in businesses that combine financial development
with positive outcomes for the Amazon forest and its populations. Its public
material covers sociobioeconomy, restoration, ingredients and products,
financial solutions, logistics, climate finance, and local value chains.

The organization was conceived and is coordinated by Idesam. AMAZ combines an
investment function with a customized acceleration program; businesses that
are only accelerated are not automatically treated as investments.

## Portfolio signals

AMAZ reported 21 impact businesses in its portfolio in May 2025. Its 2025 call
offered acceleration and investment of up to BRL 1 million. AmidoMato joined
the portfolio through a seed round in July 2026, closing the first investment
fund's five-year cycle. The official announcement says no new business call is
planned for 2026.
