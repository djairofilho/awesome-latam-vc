# Eurofarma Ventures

Eurofarma Ventures is Eurofarma's corporate venture fund for biotechnology and
breakthrough drug research and development.

## Investment profile

- **Website:** https://eurofarma.com/eurofarma-ventures
- **Fund type:** Corporate venture capital fund
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes
- **Open to external founders:** Yes
- **Stage at entry:** Early discovery and development
- **Follow-on stages:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Focus:** Biotechnology, drug discovery, and advanced therapies
- **Geography:** Global
- **Initial check:** Up to USD 5 million per company over its investment cycle
- **Investment role:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Business models:** Biotechnology companies developing drugs and therapies
- **Portfolio size:** 6 companies displayed on the official website
- **Selected companies:** Abcuro, Gen-t, Walden Biosciences, Pretzel Therapeutics, and Psithera
- **Submit a startup:** https://eurofarma.com/eurofarma-ventures

## Declared thesis

The fund targets biotechnology companies working at the frontier of drug
discovery and development. Its official material cites autoimmune, cancer,
cell and gene, immunological, inflammatory, renal, rare, neurodegenerative, and
infectious disease programs, as well as enabling technologies such as
artificial intelligence.

Eurofarma created the vehicle within its Innovation area with a USD 100 million
capital commitment. The company's 2026 integrated report says the fund reviewed
its strategy in 2025 and plans a hybrid model with greater emphasis on
partnerships. Eurofarma Ventures remains distinct from EurON Ventures, the
company's separate early-stage healthtech fund.

## Portfolio signals

The current official page displays six companies: Abcuro, Gen-t, Walden
Biosciences, Pretzel Therapeutics, Psithera, and Volnay. This count is limited
to the named company portfolio and does not include investments in other funds.
