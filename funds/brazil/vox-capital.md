# Vox Capital

Vox Capital is a Brazilian impact-investment manager backing technology
companies that combine scalable growth with measurable social or environmental
outcomes.

## Investment profile

- **Website:** https://novo.voxcapital.com.br/
- **Stage at entry:** Seed, Series A, and Series B
- **Follow-on stages:** Growth co-investment opportunities may be available
- **Focus:** Impact technology in health, education, financial inclusion,
  sustainable agriculture, climate, energy transition, and smart cities
- **Geography:** Brazil
- **Initial check:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Investment role:** Not publicly disclosed; the firm also structures
  co-investment opportunities at later stages
- **Business models:** Technology-based and scalable impact businesses
- **Portfolio size:** 39 companies listed on the official portfolio page at the
  verification date
- **Selected companies:** Celcoin, Gênica, Magnamed, Sanar, and Traive
- **Submit a startup:** https://us-dealflow.edda.co/form/form-widget/embed/JHCd0OfYurL3mBrBzhsV/vox_capital

## Declared thesis

Vox invests in technology companies with intentional, measurable positive
impact. Its Seed vehicles are sector-specific, while its flagship funds focus on
companies with traction in Series A and B. The current thesis includes social
and environmental verticals.

## Portfolio signals

The local project dataset also contains 39 companies associated with Vox.
Healthcare and climate are the largest observed groups. This distribution
describes the collected portfolio, not the full investment mandate.
