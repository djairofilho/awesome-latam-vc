# Fundo inovabra I

Fundo inovabra I is a proprietary corporate venture capital vehicle of
Bradesco, operated within the bank's inovabra ventures investment platform.

## Investment profile

- **Website:** https://www.inovabra.com.br/content/inovabra/startups.html
- **Fund type:** Corporate venture capital
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes
- **Open to external founders:** Yes
- **Stage at entry:** Series A and Series B
- **Follow-on stages:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Focus:** Technology across financial and non-financial sectors
- **Geography:** Global
- **Initial check:** From BRL 10 million
- **Investment role:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Business models:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Portfolio size:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Selected companies:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Submit a startup:** https://www.inovabra.com.br/content/inovabra/startups.html

## Declared thesis

The fund seeks companies with a well-defined value proposition and the
potential to reach global markets. Bradesco describes the vehicle as flexible
in both investment size and structure and positions its corporate resources as
levers for business development and expansion.

## Portfolio signals

The reviewed official source does not publish a complete portfolio list or
count for Fundo inovabra I. Companies connected to other inovabra programs are
not treated as investments without separate confirmation.
