# GR8 Ventures

GR8 Ventures is a Brazilian investment company backing high-growth technology
startups that have commercial traction and recurring revenue.

## Investment profile

- **Website:** https://www.gr8ventures.com.br/
- **Fund type:** Venture capital
- **Operator:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes
- **Open to external founders:** Yes
- **Stage at entry:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Follow-on stages:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Focus:** High-growth technology startups
- **Geography:** Brazil
- **Initial check:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Investment role:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Business models:** Commercial traction and preferably recurring revenue
- **Portfolio size:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Selected companies:** Leads Per Hour, Sinatra, LeverPro, Dattos, and Tevec
- **Submit a startup:** https://www.gr8ventures.com.br/

## Declared thesis

GR8 Ventures describes itself as a Brazilian investment company focused on
high-growth technology startups. It looks for businesses that already have
commercial traction and preferably recurring revenue, even when they are still
at an early point in their development.

## Portfolio signals

The official portfolio shows investments across software, data, industrial
technology, sales technology, and financial services. The website also gives
founders a direct instruction to submit a pitch deck by email.
