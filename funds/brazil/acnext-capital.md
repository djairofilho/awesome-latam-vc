# ACNEXT Capital

ACNEXT Capital is a São Paulo-based investor making minority investments in
revenue-generating private companies with technology-led growth potential in
Brazil and abroad.

## Investment profile

- **Website:** https://acnext.com/
- **Fund type:** Venture capital
- **Operator:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes
- **Open to external founders:** Yes
- **Stage at entry:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Follow-on stages:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Focus:** Technology, innovation, and preferably asset-light businesses
- **Geography:** Brazil and global
- **Initial check:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Investment role:** Minority investor
- **Business models:** Validated product-market fit, proven go-to-market, and
  existing revenue
- **Portfolio size:** Ten companies shown on the official website at the time of
  verification
- **Selected companies:** B4A, Finbits, Magnopus, Vesper Biotechnologies, and
  Zaia
- **Submit a startup:** https://acnext.com/

## Declared thesis

ACNEXT says it invests in developing private companies in Brazil and abroad.
It seeks high-growth businesses leveraged by technology and innovation, with
validated product-market fit, a proven go-to-market strategy, and revenue. The
firm describes its investments as minority positions and its sector thesis as
agnostic.

## Portfolio signals

The official portfolio includes companies in beauty technology, fintech,
biotechnology, immersive technology, and artificial intelligence. This mix is
consistent with ACNEXT's declared sector-agnostic approach.
