# SaaSholic

SaaSholic is a São Paulo-based early-stage venture capital firm investing in SaaS and B2B software companies across Latin America.

## Investment profile

- **Website:** https://www.saasholic.com/
- **Fund type:** Early-stage venture capital
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes
- **Open to external founders:** Yes
- **Stage at entry:** pre seed, seed
- **Follow-on stages:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Focus:** b2b saas, software, artificial intelligence
- **Geography:** BR, LATAM
- **Initial check:** Typically USD 250,000 at pre-seed and USD 500,000 at seed
- **Investment role:** High-conviction investor seeking to lead and support go-to-market execution
- **Business models:** Latin American SaaS, AI-SaaS and B2B software
- **Portfolio size:** 14 Fund II companies
- **Selected companies:** Sinky, HiSofi, Didit, Atlas Governance, Jusfy, Clicksign, Conta Simples, Salvy, Liquid
- **Submit a startup:** [SaaSholic](https://memo.saasholic.com/)

## Declared thesis

SaaSholic backs Latin American SaaS, AI-SaaS and B2B software founders from pre-seed through seed. It looks for initial traction and supports sales, marketing, pricing, hiring and subsequent fundraising.

## Portfolio signals

The current official site reports 14 Fund II companies and names portfolio companies. Its founder page documents direct SAFE investment, typical checks, an application route and an active investment process.
