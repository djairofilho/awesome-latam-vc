# Panvel Ventures

Panvel Ventures is Grupo Panvel's corporate venture capital program for
startups strategically connected to its health and retail businesses.

## Declared thesis

The program invests in startups that can improve Grupo Panvel's core business
or create adjacent solutions. Its three declared strategic pillars are health
and wellbeing, customer experience, and operational intelligence.

## Portfolio signals

Grupo Panvel presents connection, acceleration, experimentation, and corporate
venture capital as distinct innovation mechanisms. The reviewed official page
does not publish a separately identified Panvel Ventures portfolio.
