# Canastra Ventures

Canastra Ventures is a Brazilian pre-seed venture capital firm investing in
AI-first startups that target Brazil and have global ambitions.

## Investment profile

- **Website:** https://www.canastra.ventures/
- **Fund type:** Venture capital
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes
- **Open to external founders:** Yes
- **Stage at entry:** Pre-seed
- **Follow-on stages:** Supports preparation for Seed fundraising; follow-on
  investment is not publicly disclosed
- **Focus:** AI-first startups
- **Geography:** Brazil and global
- **Initial check:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Investment role:** Hands-on investor; lead or co-investor preference is not
  publicly disclosed
- **Business models:** Sector agnostic, provided AI is central to the product
- **Portfolio size:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Selected companies:** Not publicly disclosed on the official website
- **Submit a startup:** [Pitch Canastra Ventures](https://www.canastra.ventures/pitch-us)

## Declared thesis

Canastra Ventures invests in the first rounds of AI-first startups,
preferentially founded by Brazilians, that treat Brazil as a target market and
have global ambitions. Its pre-seed criteria include a validated MVP, early
revenue or comparable traction, a defined go-to-market strategy, and a path to
a Seed round within 24 months. The firm targets rounds of BRL 500,000 to BRL 2
million and pre-money valuations of BRL 5 million to BRL 20 million; these
figures describe the target round, not Canastra Ventures' individual check.

After investing, the firm describes a 100-day plan with weekly follow-up and
support for AI product strategy, talent acquisition, business development,
go-to-market, advisors, and Seed fundraising.

## Portfolio signals

The official website does not currently publish a portfolio list or count, and
the local project dataset has no companies associated with Canastra Ventures.
Companies participating in its AI Residency or acceleration cohorts are not
treated as investments without separate confirmation.
