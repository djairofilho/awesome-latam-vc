# Gerdau Next Ventures

Gerdau Next Ventures is Gerdau Next's corporate venture capital and startup
acceleration initiative for developing businesses adjacent to Gerdau.

## Investment profile

- **Website:** https://www2.gerdau.com.br/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2025/05/sobre_gerdaunext.pdf
- **Fund type:** Corporate venture capital
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes
- **Open to external founders:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Stage at entry:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Follow-on stages:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Focus:** Construction, mobility, sustainability, and technology
- **Geography:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Initial check:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Investment role:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Business models:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Portfolio size:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Selected companies:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Submit a startup:** Not publicly disclosed

## Declared thesis

The initiative invests in startups with high growth potential and strategic
connections to Gerdau Next's construction, mobility, sustainability, and
technology verticals. The reviewed material distinguishes the corporate venture
capital activity from Gerdau Next's broader new-business portfolio.

## Portfolio signals

The official presentation reports companies in Gerdau Next's broader portfolio
but does not provide a complete, separately identified portfolio for Gerdau
Next Ventures. Acceleration participants are not treated as investments without
separate confirmation.
