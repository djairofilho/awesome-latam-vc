# Arapy

Arapy is an impact investment vehicle managed by Fundepar for innovative
Brazilian businesses addressing the United Nations Sustainable Development
Goals.

## Investment profile

- **Website:** https://fundepar.com.br/fundos-e-programas/arapy/
- **Fund type:** Venture capital
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes
- **Open to external founders:** Yes
- **Stage at entry:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Follow-on stages:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Focus:** Positive social and environmental impact aligned with the UN
  Sustainable Development Goals
- **Geography:** Brazil
- **Initial check:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Investment role:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Business models:** Financially viable impact businesses in housing, urban
  mobility, health, education, climate, creative economy, digital
  transformation, environmental impact reduction, access to justice, and
  democracy
- **Portfolio size:** 1 publicly announced investment
- **Selected companies:** Sem Frascos
- **Submit a startup:** https://fundepar.com.br/formulario-de-inscricao-arapy/

## Declared thesis

Arapy seeks innovative businesses in an initial development phase whose value
proposition addresses at least one UN Sustainable Development Goal. It
evaluates the founding team, measurable social or environmental impact, and
the financial viability of the business model.

## Portfolio signals

Fundepar announced Sem Frascos as Arapy's first investment in May 2026. A July
2026 Fundep update states that the vehicle remained active and continued
reviewing proposals. Arapy is presented separately from its manager, Fundepar.
