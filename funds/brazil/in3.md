# IN3

IN3 is a Brazilian impact investment manager connecting investors with
innovative businesses addressing social and environmental challenges.

## Investment profile

- **Website:** https://intr3s.com.br/
- **Fund type:** Venture capital
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes
- **Open to external founders:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Stage at entry:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Follow-on stages:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Focus:** Social and environmental impact
- **Geography:** Northern and Northeastern Brazil
- **Initial check:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Investment role:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Business models:** Innovative and scalable impact businesses
- **Portfolio size:** 3 companies displayed on the official investments page
- **Selected companies:** NINA, WiseGo, and Faztudu
- **Submit a startup:** Not publicly disclosed

## Declared thesis

IN3's funds support early-stage startups with scalable solutions to social and
environmental challenges in Northern and Northeastern Brazil. Its social
themes include housing, education, health, wellbeing, citizenship, income
generation, and the creative economy. Environmental themes include food,
sanitation, sustainable consumption and agriculture, clean energy, and
sustainable tourism.

The official site describes IN3 as a manager whose funds make the investments,
but does not identify each legal vehicle. Companies and projects are kept
separate on the site, and only the three organizations on the investments page
are counted as portfolio companies.

## Portfolio signals

The official investments page lists NINA, WiseGo, and Faztudu. IN3's official
LinkedIn channel also reported activity involving the Recybacks investment
spin-off in 2025, but the company is not added to the portfolio count because
it is not displayed on the reviewed investments page.
