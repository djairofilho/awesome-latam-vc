## Declared thesis

BS2 Ventures, also described as the Fundo de CVC do BS2, is Banco BS2's
corporate venture capital fund for companies developing solutions for small
and medium-sized businesses. The official launch announced BRL 100 million in
capital and a target portfolio of 12 to 15 companies.

## Portfolio signals

At launch on November 12, 2024, Banco BS2 reported that the fund had already
made direct investments in Bloxs and Somos Young. The reviewed source does not
disclose a normalized entry stage, initial check, or founder submission route.
