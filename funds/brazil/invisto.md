# Invisto

Invisto is a venture capital investment circle in southern Brazil backing
validated startups entering the scale-up phase.

- **Website:** https://invisto.com.vc/
- **Fund type:** Co-investment fund
- **Operator:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes
- **Open to external founders:** Yes
- **Stage at entry:** Growth
- **Follow-on stages:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Focus:** Technology, software, and internet businesses
- **Geography:** Southern Brazil
- **Initial check:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Investment role:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Business models:** Validated startups in the scale-up phase
- **Portfolio size:** More than 50 businesses invested over more than 20 years,
  as reported by Invisto
- **Selected companies:** Not publicly disclosed in the reviewed text sources
- **Submit a startup:** https://invisto.com.vc/empreendedor

## Declared thesis

Invisto presents itself as a venture capital investment circle focused on
southern Brazil. Its founder route targets startups with a validated business
model that are entering the scale-up phase and seeking new investors.

## Portfolio signals

Invisto reports more than BRL 300 million invested in startups and more than 50
businesses backed over more than 20 years. Its website displays current
investments and maintains separate routes for entrepreneurs and investors.
