# Randon Ventures (RV)

Randon Ventures, now branded RV, is Randoncorp's startup investment and
acceleration company.

- **Website:** https://www.randoncorp.com/pt/blog/rv-amplia-portfolio-com-dois-novos-investimentos-em-solucoes-financeiras/
- **Fund type:** Accelerator and venture capital
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes
- **Open to external founders:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Stage at entry:** Growth
- **Follow-on stages:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Focus:** Financial solutions aligned with Randoncorp's strategic businesses
- **Geography:** Brazil
- **Initial check:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Investment role:** Lead and co-investor
- **Business models:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Portfolio size:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Selected companies:** Guarda and Sinky
- **Submit a startup:** Not publicly disclosed

## Declared thesis

RV describes its current strategy as growth-oriented and open to companies at
different stages across Brazil when they align with Randoncorp's purpose and
offer scalable strategic solutions. The reviewed current announcement centers
on financial solutions and distinguishes RV as both an investor and an
accelerator.

## Portfolio signals

In December 2025, RV announced that it led a BRL 4.5 million round in Guarda and
participated in a round for Sinky. These recent transactions show both lead and
co-investment roles but do not establish a complete portfolio size.
