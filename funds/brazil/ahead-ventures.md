# Ahead Ventures

Ahead Ventures is a Brazilian corporate venture capital manager that structures
and operates investment programs for corporations.

## Investment profile

- **Website:** https://aheadventures.com.br/
- **Fund type:** Corporate venture capital
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes
- **Open to external founders:** Yes
- **Stage at entry:** Seed and Series A
- **Follow-on stages:** Series B and Growth
- **Focus:** Sectors aligned with each corporate client's strategic thesis
- **Geography:** Brazil
- **Initial check:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Investment role:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Business models:** RetailTech, Fashion Tech, PropTech, ConstruTech, AgriTech,
  Fintech, Marketing and AdTech, decarbonization, aerospace, edge AI, and edge
  IoT
- **Portfolio size:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Selected companies:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Submit a startup:** mailto:contato@aheadventures.com.br

## Declared thesis

Ahead develops corporate venture capital strategies around the priorities of
each client. Its current mandates mainly target Series A and Series B, with
some Seed investments; the client page also identifies an active Seed and
Series A fund and a mandate extending to Series C.

## Portfolio signals

The official client page identifies RX Ventures as an active FIP operated by
Ahead and describes other corporate investment mandates. The reviewed sources
do not publish a consolidated company portfolio or official portfolio count.
