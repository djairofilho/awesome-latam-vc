# Invest Tech

Invest Tech is a Brazilian private equity and venture capital manager investing
in technology-enabled companies that digitize the country's economy.

## Investment profile

- **Website:** https://investtech.com.br/
- **Fund type:** Venture capital
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes
- **Open to external founders:** Yes
- **Stage at entry:** Seed
- **Follow-on stages:** Series A
- **Focus:** Technology-enabled B2B companies
- **Geography:** Brazil
- **Initial check:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Investment role:** Co-investor at Series A
- **Business models:** B2B and B2B2C across IT and telecom, AgriTech, Cleantech,
  SaaS, Fintech, HealthTech, EdTech, and cybersecurity
- **Portfolio size:** 28 investments
- **Selected companies:** Actiz, Monuv, Rúmina, GoBots, and Logcomex
- **Submit a startup:** https://investtech.com.br/en/#contato

## Declared thesis

Invest Tech backs validated Seed-stage companies whose technology improves
performance and market positioning, with a preference for B2B and B2B2C
models. Its venture strategy allows co-investment in Series A.

## Portfolio signals

The official website reports 28 investments across five funds. Its current
portfolio includes companies in laboratory software, artificial intelligence,
agribusiness, e-commerce automation, logistics, cloud infrastructure, and data
analytics.
