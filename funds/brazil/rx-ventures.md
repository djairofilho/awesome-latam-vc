# RX Ventures

RX Ventures is Lojas Renner S.A.'s corporate venture capital vehicle for
startups shaping the future of fashion, retail, and lifestyle.

## Investment profile

- **Website:** https://www.lojasrennersa.com.br/rx-ventures/
- **Fund type:** Corporate venture capital
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes
- **Open to external founders:** Yes
- **Stage at entry:** Seed and Series A
- **Follow-on stages:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Focus:** Fashion, retail, lifestyle, customer experience, and responsible brands
- **Geography:** Global
- **Initial check:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Investment role:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Business models:** Fashiontech, retailtech, fintech, supply chain, commerce, and sustainability solutions
- **Portfolio size:** 5 companies displayed on the official page
- **Selected companies:** Topsort, Connectly, Radar, Klavi, and Logstore
- **Submit a startup:** https://airtable.com/app4qXQkm1B3hEZrh/pagujtJRtVULaeKoN/form

## Declared thesis

The vehicle invests in companies with traction, execution capacity, and
scaling potential across fashion and lifestyle, customer experience, and
responsible brands. The detailed themes include new materials, data-driven
design, supply chains, store technology, omnichannel commerce, financial
services, circularity, traceability, and value-chain efficiency.

RX Ventures is a corporate fund of Lojas Renner S.A. The official team page
lists both Lojas Renner professionals and investment professionals from Ahead
Ventures. It does not publicly define Ahead Ventures' formal legal mandate for
the vehicle, so no management relationship beyond the disclosed team is
inferred.

## Portfolio signals

The official page identifies five investments and dates them from July 2022
through July 2024. Lojas Renner's 2025 sustainability disclosure confirms that
RX Ventures continues to supervise and guide innovation and venture capital
investments.
