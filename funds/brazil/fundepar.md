# Fundepar

Fundepar is a Brazilian venture capital manager supporting innovative,
high-growth businesses through direct investments and dedicated vehicles.

## Investment profile

- **Website:** https://fundepar.com.br/
- **Fund type:** Venture capital
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes
- **Open to external founders:** Yes
- **Stage at entry:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Follow-on stages:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Focus:** Technology, science-based innovation, and positive social or
  environmental impact
- **Geography:** Brazil
- **Initial check:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Investment role:** Active management support
- **Business models:** Technology-based and impact businesses, with preference
  for academic origins in some vehicles
- **Portfolio size:** 27 companies publicly reported as 14 direct investments
  and 13 Seed4Science investments, with data updated through January 2024
- **Selected companies:** Sem Frascos
- **Submit a startup:** https://fundepar.com.br/fundos-e-programas/formulario-de-inscricao-seed4science/

## Declared thesis

Fundepar provides entrepreneurial capital and active management support to
innovative companies with high growth potential. Its vehicles target
early-stage technology and impact businesses, including academic spinouts and
companies addressing relevant social and environmental problems.

## Portfolio signals

Fundep reports 14 companies invested in directly and 13 through Seed4Science,
with those figures updated through January 2024. Fundepar announced the first
Arapy investment, Sem Frascos, in May 2026. Arapy is documented in its own
profile because it is a vehicle managed by Fundepar, not an alias for the
manager.
