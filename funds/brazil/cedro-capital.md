# Cedro Capital

Cedro Capital is an independent Brasília-based asset manager investing venture
capital in high-growth technology companies, with a focus on Central Brazil.

## Investment profile

- **Website:** https://www.cedrocapital.com/
- **Fund type:** Venture capital
- **Operator:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes
- **Open to external founders:** Yes
- **Stage at entry:** Seed
- **Follow-on stages:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Focus:** Technology, GovTech, and high-growth companies
- **Geography:** Brazil, with a stated focus on Central Brazil
- **Initial check:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Investment role:** Lead or co-investor
- **Business models:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Portfolio size:** 30 investments reported by Cedro Capital in June 2025
- **Selected companies:** Jovens Gênios, Kinebot, GRTS Digital, StartGi, and
  Prosas
- **Submit a startup:** https://www.cedrocapital.com/

## Declared thesis

Cedro Capital describes itself as an independent asset and fund manager based
in Brasília. Its venture capital activity backs innovative, high-growth
technology businesses. Its current Fundo GovTech (GovTech Fund), co-managed
with KPTL, invests in technology that improves public services across multiple
verticals.

## Portfolio signals

Cedro's official releases document current deployment: the GovTech Fund led a
BRL 11.8 million Seed round in Jovens Gênios in March 2026 and announced a BRL
3 million investment in Kinebot in February 2026. The releases also identify
GRTS Digital as Cedro Capital's thirtieth investment.
