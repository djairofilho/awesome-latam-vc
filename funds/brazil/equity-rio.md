# Equity Rio

Equity Rio, also known as Equity Rio Investimentos, is a Brazilian investment
and fundraising firm that leads early-stage rounds and may invest its own
capital alongside other investors.

## Investment profile

- **Website:** https://www.equityrio.com.br/
- **Fund type:** Co-investment fund
- **Operator:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes, selectively alongside its fundraising
  activity
- **Open to external founders:** Yes
- **Stage at entry:** Angel, Pre-seed, and Seed
- **Follow-on stages:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Focus:** Early-stage technology startups and small and medium-sized
  businesses
- **Geography:** Brazil
- **Initial check:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Investment role:** Lead or co-investor
- **Business models:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Portfolio size:** More than 30 companies reported in 2026
- **Selected companies:** Aviva Fintech, Mate Guayrá, Electy, Nuu, and SaveLivez
- **Submit a startup:** https://www.equityrio.com.br/lpcopiloto-fundraising

## Declared thesis

Equity Rio combines investment activity with paid fundraising and advisory
services. Its 2026 materials say the firm works most frequently with Angel,
Pre-seed, and Seed equity rounds and may invest directly as a lead or
co-investor when there is strategic alignment, typically contributing 1% to 5%
of the amount raised.

## Portfolio signals

The firm reports more than BRL 15 million invested and more than 30 portfolio
companies. Its current materials identify a BRL 3 million bridge round for
Aviva Fintech and a BRL 1.5 million round for Mate Guayrá as recent operations
that it led and invested in.
