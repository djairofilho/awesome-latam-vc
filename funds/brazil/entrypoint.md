# Entrypoint

Entrypoint is a Brazilian pre-seed venture capital firm investing in
technology startups with a functioning MVP and initial clients or proofs of
concept.

## Investment profile

- **Website:** https://entrypoint.vc/
- **Fund type:** Venture capital
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes
- **Open to external founders:** Yes
- **Stage at entry:** Pre-seed
- **Follow-on stages:** Helps companies prepare for their next funding round;
  follow-on investment is not publicly disclosed
- **Focus:** Technology startups solving concrete market problems
- **Geography:** Brazil
- **Initial check:** Typically USD 50,000 to USD 150,000
- **Investment role:** Hands-on partner; lead or co-investor preference is not
  publicly disclosed
- **Business models:** Not publicly disclosed; technology must be central to
  solving a clear customer problem
- **Portfolio size:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Selected companies:** Not publicly disclosed on the official website
- **Submit a startup:** [Entrypoint application](https://airtable.com/appD4x694mYx8HTKc/pagi9EoW9lTCFKC7U/form)

## Declared thesis

Entrypoint partners with Brazilian technology entrepreneurs from the
beginning. It invests at pre-seed in companies that already have a functioning
MVP and initial clients or proofs of concept. The firm prioritizes
execution-driven founders with technical and domain expertise who use
technology to solve a clearly understood market problem.

The firm describes active support with customer-profile definition, value-chain
mapping, early product development, go-to-market and business-model strategy,
client introductions, and preparation for the next funding round.
