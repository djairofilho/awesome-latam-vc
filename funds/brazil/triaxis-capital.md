# Triaxis Capital

Triaxis Capital is a Brazilian venture capital manager investing in
technology-based companies through dedicated investment vehicles.

## Investment profile

- **Website:** https://www.triaxiscapital.com/
- **Fund type:** Venture capital
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes
- **Open to external founders:** Yes
- **Stage at entry:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Follow-on stages:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Focus:** Innovative technology-based companies
- **Geography:** Brazil
- **Initial check:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Investment role:** Active investor providing governance and strategic
  support
- **Business models:** Innovative and scalable technology-based businesses
- **Portfolio size:** 42 companies
- **Selected companies:** Not publicly disclosed in accessible text
- **Submit a startup:** https://www.triaxiscapital.com/#contato

## Declared thesis

Triaxis seeks technology startups outside conventional deal flow and combines
capital with structured governance, strategic support, and access to investor,
partner, and market networks. The manager states that it supports startups
across phases but does not publish entry and follow-on stages precisely enough
to normalize them.

## Portfolio signals

The official website reports three active funds, 42 investees, and BRL 550
million under management. FIP Nordeste Capital Semente is documented in its
own profile because it is a vehicle managed by Triaxis, not an alias for the
manager.
