# Vivo Ventures

Vivo Ventures is Vivo's corporate venture capital vehicle for later-stage
strategic investments and is managed in coordination with Wayra Brasil.

## Investment profile

- **Website:** https://www.telefonica.com.br/inovacao
- **Fund type:** Corporate venture capital
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes
- **Open to external founders:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Stage at entry:** Series A, Series B, and Growth
- **Follow-on stages:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Focus:** Education, energy, health, financial services, smart home, and entertainment
- **Geography:** Brazil
- **Initial check:** Up to BRL 30 million on average
- **Investment role:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Business models:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Portfolio size:** 14 startups reported by Vivo in February 2026
- **Selected companies:** Not publicly disclosed in the reviewed sources
- **Submit a startup:** Not publicly disclosed

## Declared thesis

Vivo Ventures invests from Series A onward in companies that can advance Vivo's
new-business strategy, support strategic partnerships, and enable entry into
new markets. Vivo distinguishes the vehicle from Wayra, which covers earlier
stages, while stating that the two operate in an integrated manner.

## Portfolio signals

Vivo reported more than BRL 220 million invested in 14 startups and seven
investments completed during 2025. The reviewed sources do not provide a
complete name-by-name portfolio for the vehicle.
