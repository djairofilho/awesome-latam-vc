# FIR Capital

FIR Capital, also known as FIR Capital Partners, is a Brazilian alternative
investment manager that manages venture capital and private equity funds and
supports high-growth companies.

## Investment profile

- **Website:** https://www.fircapital.com/
- **Fund type:** Venture capital and private equity
- **Operator:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes
- **Open to external founders:** Yes
- **Stage at entry:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Follow-on stages:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Focus:** Innovation, sustainability, and high-growth companies
- **Geography:** Brazil
- **Initial check:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Investment role:** Active management support
- **Business models:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Portfolio size:** More than 50 companies invested, as reported by FIR
  Capital
- **Selected companies:** ISA Saúde and SolarVolt
- **Submit a startup:** https://airtable.com/appYsb0YkUNQcLbbp/pagLH7iG6DmlJHvTk/form

## Declared thesis

FIR Capital describes itself as an alternative investment firm founded in
1999. It manages venture capital and private equity funds, invests in
high-growth companies, and provides active management support, knowledge, and
capital-markets access. Its website reports more than BRL 1 billion in assets
under management, more than ten funds structured, and more than 50 companies
invested.

## Portfolio signals

The current official portfolio identifies ISA Saúde as an investment associated
with venture capital and private equity. LAVCA also reported FIR Capital's
direct investment in SolarVolt. These independent signals support direct
company deployment, while the firm's stage and check size remain marked as not
publicly disclosed.
