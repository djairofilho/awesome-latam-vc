# RD Saúde Ventures

RD Saúde Ventures is RD Saúde's corporate innovation and venture investment
program for healthcare, wellbeing, and pharmacy retail.

## Investment profile

- **Website:** https://rdsaude.com.br/rdsaudeventures/
- **Fund type:** Corporate venture capital
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes
- **Open to external founders:** Yes
- **Stage at entry:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Follow-on stages:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Focus:** Health, wellbeing, pharmacy retail, and customer experience
- **Geography:** Brazil
- **Initial check:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Investment role:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Business models:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Portfolio size:** 10 startups reported on the official page
- **Selected companies:** Not publicly disclosed on the official page
- **Submit a startup:** https://rdsaude.com.br/rdsaudeventures/

## Declared thesis

The program seeks solutions for integrated health, wellbeing, pharmacy retail,
customer experience, omnichannel operations, delivery, products, packaging,
beauty, and marketplaces. RD Saúde distinguishes corporate venture investment
from commercial partnerships, fast-track contracting, and pilot projects.

## Portfolio signals

The official page reports 10 invested startups, more than 30 pilots, and more
than 100 startup connections. Only the first figure is treated as portfolio
size; pilots and connections are not counted as investments.
