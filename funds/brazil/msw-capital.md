# MSW Capital

MSW Capital is a Brazilian venture capital manager connecting technology
startups with corporate investors through corporate venture capital funds.

## Investment profile

- **Website:** https://www.mswcapital.com.br/
- **Fund type:** Venture capital
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes
- **Open to external founders:** Yes
- **Stage at entry:** Seed
- **Follow-on stages:** Series A
- **Focus:** Technology startups aligned with corporate innovation theses
- **Geography:** Brazil
- **Initial check:** BRL 2 million to BRL 15 million
- **Investment role:** Minority investor
- **Business models:** InsurTech, EnergyTech, aerospace, AgriTech, cargo and
  logistics, and adjacent technology markets
- **Portfolio size:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Selected companies:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Submit a startup:** mailto:meudeck@msw.com.br

## Declared thesis

Through MSW Multicorp II, the manager seeks technology startups raising Seed or
Series A rounds that have strategic fit with its corporate investors. The fund
takes minority stakes and combines capital with commercial connections,
product co-development, governance support, mentorship, and later fundraising
support.

## Portfolio signals

The reviewed official fund page names Baterias Moura, BB Seguros, AgeRio, and
Embraer as corporate investors and publishes a live deck-submission channel.
It does not state an official startup portfolio count or identify a current
consolidated list of investees.
