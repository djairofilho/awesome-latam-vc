# Scale-Up Ventures

Scale-Up Ventures is an investment organization focused on Endeavor network companies in Brazil.

## Investment profile

- **Website:** https://endeavor.org.br/scale-up-ventures
- **Fund type:** Co-investment fund
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes
- **Open to external founders:** No
- **Stage at entry:** Seed and Series A
- **Follow-on stages:** Not publicly disclosed in the reviewed sources
- **Focus:** Endeavor network companies
- **Geography:** Brazil
- **Initial check:** Not publicly disclosed in the reviewed sources
- **Investment role:** Passive co-investor in rounds led by qualified institutional investors
- **Business models:** Not publicly disclosed in the reviewed sources
- **Portfolio size:** 5 companies captured in the local project dataset; this is not an official portfolio count
- **Selected companies:** Avec, Hashdex, Letz, Locaweb, Traive
- **Submit a startup:** No direct application; investment is limited to companies actively supported by Scale-Up Endeavor

## Declared thesis

Scale-Up Ventures is Endeavor Brazil's venture capital fund. It acts as a
passive co-investor in early-stage rounds led by qualified institutional
investors and only considers companies actively supported by a Scale-Up
Endeavor program.

## Portfolio signals

The local project dataset contains 5 companies associated with Scale-Up Ventures. Its most frequent sector labels are Tecnologia e serviços digitais, AgFintech / Crédito, and its most frequent company countries are Brazil, Brasil / EUA. These are observations from an incomplete local collection, not the fund's declared mandate or official portfolio size.
