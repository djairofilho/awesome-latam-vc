- **Fund type:** Co-investment fund

## Declared thesis

Scale-Up Ventures is Endeavor Brazil's venture capital fund. It acts as a
passive co-investor in early-stage rounds led by qualified institutional
investors and only considers companies actively supported by a Scale-Up
Endeavor program.
