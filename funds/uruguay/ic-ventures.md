# IC Ventures

IC Ventures is a Montevideo-based private fund investing in Latin American technology startups at seed and pre-Series A.

## Investment profile

- **Website:** https://ic-ventures.vc/
- **Fund type:** Private venture capital fund
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes
- **Open to external founders:** Yes
- **Stage at entry:** seed, series a
- **Follow-on stages:** Exceptional strategic participation
- **Focus:** technology, artificial intelligence, fintech
- **Geography:** UY, AR, LATAM
- **Initial check:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Investment role:** Hands-on investor; may lead financing processes
- **Business models:** Preference for B2B and recurring-revenue businesses
- **Portfolio size:** 11 companies named on the reviewed official page
- **Selected companies:** Paganza, Tryolabs, MonkeyLearn, NocNoc, Nubimetrics
- **Submit a startup:** [IC Ventures](https://ic-ventures.vc/#contacto)

## Declared thesis

The fund seeks technology-centered companies with disruptive projects, rapid scaling potential and execution-focused teams. It reports a non-exclusive preference for B2B, recurring revenue, artificial intelligence and fintech.

## Portfolio signals

The official site says the fund is fully active, reports recent seed and Series A investments, and lists investments in Uruguay and Argentina. Follow-on participation is exceptional rather than the standard model.
