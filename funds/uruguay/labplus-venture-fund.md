# LAB+ Venture Fund

LAB+ Venture Fund is a Montevideo life-sciences vehicle created through collaboration between Institut Pasteur de Montevideo and FICUS Advisory.

## Investment profile

- **Website:** https://labplus.uy/
- **Fund type:** Venture fund with company-building support
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes
- **Open to external founders:** Yes
- **Stage at entry:** not disclosed
- **Follow-on stages:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Focus:** life sciences, one health, biotechnology
- **Geography:** UY, GLOBAL
- **Initial check:** USD 750,000 committed to each of the first four startups
- **Investment role:** Investor and scientific company-building partner
- **Business models:** Patentable science-based projects and startups
- **Portfolio size:** 4 companies on the reviewed official portfolio
- **Selected companies:** B4-RNA, GUSKA, LoCBio, Scaffold Biotech
- **Submit a startup:** [LAB+ Venture Fund](https://labplus.uy/start-ups/)

## Declared thesis

The fund finances life-sciences projects and startups under a One Health approach, covering human, animal and environmental health. LAB+ periodically runs international calls and also accepts preliminary contact when no call is open.

## Portfolio signals

ANII identifies LAB+ Venture Fund as a fund distinct from the LAB+ Company Builder operating role. LAB+ reports that its first capitalization round closed in February 2024 and committed USD 750,000 to each of four portfolio startups.
