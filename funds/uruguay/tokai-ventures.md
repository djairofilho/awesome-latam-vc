# Tokai Ventures

Tokai Ventures is a Montevideo-based seed and venture capital fund investing across Uruguay and international markets.

## Investment profile

- **Website:** https://www.tokaiventures.com/
- **Fund type:** Seed and venture capital
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes
- **Open to external founders:** Yes
- **Stage at entry:** seed
- **Follow-on stages:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Focus:** proptech, entertainment, education, biotechnology, impact
- **Geography:** UY, AR, US, IL, LATAM
- **Initial check:** USD 30,000 to USD 1,000,000
- **Investment role:** Investor, mentor and board participant
- **Business models:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Portfolio size:** 23 companies reported by ANII
- **Selected companies:** Woow, Infocasas, Rural
- **Submit a startup:** [Tokai Ventures](https://anii.org.uy/emprendimientos/fondos-de-capital-riesgo/169/tokai-ventures/)

## Declared thesis

Tokai focuses on e-commerce, proptech and agtech while considering experienced founders in other verticals. The 2025 URUCAP directory also lists entertainment, metaverse, education, biotechnology and impact.
