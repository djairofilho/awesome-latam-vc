# Primary X

Primary X is A3 Mercados' Argentina-based corporate venture capital unit for early-stage fintech, crypto and agrifintech startups.

## Investment profile

- **Website:** https://pmyx.com.ar/
- **Fund type:** Corporate venture capital
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes
- **Open to external founders:** Yes
- **Stage at entry:** pre seed, seed, series a
- **Follow-on stages:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Focus:** fintech, cryptocurrency, agritech
- **Geography:** AR, UY
- **Initial check:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Investment role:** Strategic corporate investor and operating partner
- **Business models:** Fintech, crypto and agrifintech for capital markets
- **Portfolio size:** Multiple startups; no official total disclosed
- **Selected companies:** Origino, Skyblue Analytics
- **Submit a startup:** [Primary X](https://pmyx.com.ar/#aplicar)

## Declared thesis

Primary X invests in early-stage startups developing novel businesses and technologies for capital markets. It combines capital with planning, networking, partnerships, legal and financial support.

## Portfolio signals

The current official site states that Primary X has invested in multiple startups and accepts founder applications. A3 Mercados' official financial statements identify Primary X S.A.U. as an Argentina investment company and record investments in Origino and Skyblue Analytics during 2025.
