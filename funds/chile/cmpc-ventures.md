# CMPC Ventures

CMPC Ventures is the corporate venture capital program of Chile-based forestry and materials company CMPC.

## Investment profile

- **Website:** https://www.cmpc.com/innovacion/cmpc-ventures/
- **Fund type:** Corporate venture capital
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes
- **Open to external founders:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Stage at entry:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Follow-on stages:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Focus:** Sustainable packaging, construction, wood applications, biomaterials, digital solutions, and circular business models
- **Geography:** Global
- **Initial check:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Investment role:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Business models:** Technologies and business models connected to fiber, forests, and renewable materials
- **Portfolio size:** Seven companies listed on the reviewed portfolio page
- **Selected companies:** Modvion, Pulpex, Boxia, Strong by Form, and Rubi
- **Submit a startup:** Not publicly disclosed

## Declared thesis

CMPC Ventures invests in technologies and entrepreneurs from any country whose work connects strategically with CMPC's fiber and forestry capabilities. Its themes include sustainable packaging and construction, wood applications, lignin and nanocellulose biomaterials, and circular digital solutions.

## Portfolio signals

The official portfolio contains companies working on engineered wood, fiber packaging, biomaterials, and sustainable manufacturing. A May 2025 announcement confirms a direct investment in Rubi.
