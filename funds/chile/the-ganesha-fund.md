# The Ganesha Fund

The Ganesha Fund is an early-stage biotech fund operated by Chile-based The Ganesha Lab for startups originating in Latin America.

## Investment profile

- **Website:** https://theganeshafund.decilehub.com/
- **Fund type:** Venture capital
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes
- **Open to external founders:** Yes
- **Stage at entry:** Pre-seed and Seed
- **Follow-on stages:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Focus:** Biotech, human health, planetary health, agtech, foodtech, and medtech
- **Geography:** Latin America
- **Initial check:** Average USD 250,000
- **Investment role:** Direct investor and co-investor
- **Business models:** Scalable biotech business models
- **Portfolio size:** 20+ investments disclosed by the fund
- **Selected companies:** Delee, TELL, and Amplify Dynamics
- **Current activity:** Active investment pipeline in 2026
- **Submit a startup:** https://theganeshafund.decilehub.com/

## Declared thesis

The fund backs pre-seed and seed biotech startups originating in Latin America across human health, planetary health, agriculture, food, and medical technology. The Ganesha Lab is the Chile-based operator; The Ganesha Lab Fund I LP is the separately identified Delaware investment vehicle.

## Portfolio signals

The official fund landing reports 20+ investments and a 5x MOIC over 3 years. Official and institutional sources identify direct investments in Delee and Amplify Dynamics, a co-investment in TELL, and an active 2026 pipeline. These are observed portfolio and activity signals, not an expansion of the declared thesis.
