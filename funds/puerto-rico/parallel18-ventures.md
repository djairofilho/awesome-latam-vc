# Parallel18 Ventures

Parallel18 Ventures operates Puerto Rico's Venture Capital Access Program for
seed and early-stage companies building on the island.

## Investment profile

- **Website:** https://parallel18.com/vcap/
- **Fund type:** Public-private co-investment venture program
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes
- **Open to external founders:** Yes
- **Stage at entry:** Pre-seed, Seed, and Series A
- **Follow-on stages:** Up to Series A
- **Focus:** Sector agnostic, with priority industries strategic to Puerto Rico
- **Geography:** Puerto Rico
- **Initial check:** USD 250,000 to USD 2.5 million; about USD 500,000 initially
- **Investment role:** Minority equity co-investor with a 1:1 private match
- **Business models:** Scalable seed and early-stage companies
- **Portfolio size:** Not publicly disclosed on the reviewed page
- **Selected companies:** Not publicly disclosed on the reviewed page
- **Submit a startup:** https://parallel18.com/vcap/

## Declared thesis

VCAP invests alongside accredited private investors in companies based in
Puerto Rico or meeting disclosed founder, ownership, operation, or relocation
criteria. Eligible instruments include preferred or common equity, convertible
notes and SAFEs.

## Portfolio signals

The current page accepts applications continuously and describes quarterly
investment decisions, a 1:1 private-capital match and follow-on capacity up to
USD 2.5 million.
