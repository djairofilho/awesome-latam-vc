# ATO Ventures

ATO Ventures is a Puerto Rico-based pre-seed and seed investor backing
technology companies in the United States and Latin America.

## Investment profile

- **Website:** https://atoventures.com/
- **Fund type:** Venture capital
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes
- **Open to external founders:** Yes
- **Stage at entry:** Pre-seed and Seed
- **Follow-on stages:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Focus:** Technology, health and wellness, and digital media
- **Geography:** United States and Latin America, with headquarters in Puerto Rico
- **Initial check:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Investment role:** Earliest-stage direct investor
- **Business models:** Technology startups
- **Portfolio size:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Selected companies:** iSono Health and Quick Balance are identified in current founder accounts
- **Submit a startup:** https://www.validstartup.com/

## Declared thesis

ATO Ventures invests at the earliest stages and uses market validation testing
to assess demand before a company commits heavily to product development. The
manager describes a global mandate with primary service to the United States
and Latin America.

## Portfolio signals

Current manager-authored pages state that every ATO Ventures investment made
since Valid Startup was developed has passed through that process and include
accounts from founders of funded portfolio companies.
