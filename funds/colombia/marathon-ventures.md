# Marathon Ventures

Marathon Ventures backs early-stage founders building B2B technology companies
in Latin America.

- **Fund type:** Venture capital
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes
- **Open to external founders:** Yes
- **Stage at entry:** Pre-seed and Seed
- **Follow-on stages:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Focus:** B2B technology
- **Geography:** Latin America
- **Initial check:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Investment role:** Active earliest-stage investor
- **Business models:** B2B technology products for emerging markets
- **Portfolio size:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Selected companies:** Tul, Extendeal, Maqui, Vaas, Bacu, and Vest

## Declared thesis

Marathon Ventures describes itself as a revenue-focused venture firm investing
at the earliest stage. It backs product builders redesigning industries in
emerging markets and supports portfolio founders through its Latin American
business network.
