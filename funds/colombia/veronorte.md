# Veronorte

Veronorte is a thesis-driven investment organization that backs venture funds
and companies connected to sustainable development and wellbeing in Latin
America.

## Investment profile

- **Website:** https://veronorte.com/
- **Fund type:** Fund investor and direct investor
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes
- **Open to external founders:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Stage at entry:** Multi-stage
- **Follow-on stages:** Not publicly disclosed in the reviewed sources
- **Focus:** Sustainability and wellbeing
- **Geography:** Latin America
- **Initial check:** Not publicly disclosed in the reviewed sources
- **Investment role:** Direct investor and co-investor alongside global venture capital firms; also invests as a limited partner in funds
- **Business models:** Companies related to energy transition, agriculture, mobility, financial services, healthcare, and education
- **Portfolio size:** Not publicly disclosed as a single current official count
- **Selected companies:** Cheetah, Jüsto, and Shiru
- **Submit a startup:** Not publicly disclosed

## Declared thesis

Veronorte says it invests in and alongside leading global venture funds and
directly in early- and growth-stage companies. Its two declared thesis pillars
are sustainable development and sustainable wellbeing.
