# InQlab

InQlab is a Colombia-based investment vehicle backing technology companies
with growth potential in Colombian and Latin American markets.

## Investment profile

- **Website:** https://www.inqlab.co/
- **Fund type:** Venture capital investment vehicle
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes
- **Open to external founders:** Yes
- **Stage at entry:** Seed through Series B
- **Follow-on stages:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Focus:** Sector agnostic, with technology enabling scalable growth
- **Geography:** Colombia and Latin America
- **Initial check:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Investment role:** Strategic and financial investor
- **Business models:** Companies with product-market fit and regional potential
- **Portfolio size:** Not publicly disclosed as an active total
- **Submit a startup:** https://www.inqlab.co/

## Declared thesis

The vehicle seeks effective founding teams building disruptive technology
companies. It prioritizes businesses whose main market is Latin America and
that see Colombia as a relevant market for near-term growth.
