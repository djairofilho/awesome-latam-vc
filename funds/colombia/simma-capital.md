# Simma Capital

Simma Capital invests in early-stage technology startups connected to Latin
America.

## Investment profile

- **Website:** https://www.simmacapital.com/
- **Fund type:** Venture capital
- **Direct startup investment:** Yes
- **Open to external founders:** Yes
- **Stage at entry:** Pre-seed and Seed
- **Follow-on stages:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Focus:** Scalable technology and fintech
- **Geography:** Colombia and Latin America
- **Initial check:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Investment role:** Active investor
- **Business models:** Technology-enabled companies
- **Portfolio size:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Selected companies:** Akua, Unergy, Mipaquete.com, and Ruedata
- **Submit a startup:** https://www.simmacapital.com/

## Declared thesis

Simma Capital says it backs early-stage startups with strong teams, scalable
models, sound economics, and a connection to Latin America. Its strategies
include SIMMA VENTURES, an angel fund for pre-seed startups in Colombia, and
Simma Fintech +, which focuses on early-stage financial technology.

## Portfolio signals

The official site presents a current direct portfolio. Bancóldex and Ruta N
also announced an investment in Simma Fintech + in April 2025, providing a
dated institutional activity signal without being used as the discovery source.
