# Angel networks

Angel networks connect founders with individual investors who invest their own
capital. They are listed separately because a network is not the same as a
venture capital fund, even when its members invest together.

## Anjos do Brasil

- **Type:** Nonprofit angel-investment ecosystem organization and network
- **Website:** https://anjosdobrasil.net/
- **Geography:** Brazil
- **Founder route:** https://anjosdobrasil.net/submeter-startup/

Anjos do Brasil connects entrepreneurs with angel investors and provides
education, mentoring, events, and ecosystem advocacy. Its official materials
state that the organization does not invest directly or manage third-party
funds; investment decisions and negotiations are made by investors in the
network.

### Sources

- [About Anjos do Brasil](https://anjosdobrasil.net/sobre-nos/)
- [Submit a startup](https://anjosdobrasil.net/submeter-startup/)
- [About us in English and operating disclaimer](https://anjosdobrasil.net/about-us-in-english/)

## BR Angels

- **Type:** Private angel-investment network
- **Website:** https://www.brangels.global/
- **Geography:** Brazil, with a stated Latin American and global outlook
- **Founder route:** https://app.pipefy.com/public/form/sevD52HK

BR Angels was formed in 2019 and brings together CEOs and senior executives who
provide capital, mentoring, and professional expertise. Its founder materials
state an initial interest in B2B, B2B2C, and B2C businesses and describe a
portfolio of more than 30 startups.

### Sources

- [BR Angels startup journey and application](https://startup.brangels.com.br/)
- [Official startup application](https://app.pipefy.com/public/form/sevD52HK)

**Last verified:** 2026-07-26

## GVAngels

- **Type:** Alumni angel-investment network
- **Website:** https://gvangels.com.br/
- **Geography:** Brazil
- **Founder route:** https://app.dealum.com/#/company/application/new/53851/usveyzos5n2gm9hdm324ngpeix7uu9g7

Founded in 2017 by FGV alumni, GVAngels describes itself as an early-stage angel
investment network. Its official material says most invested companies are in
Pre-seed, Angel, or Seed rounds. Startups apply through the network's linked
Dealum form.

### Sources

- [About GVAngels](https://gvangels.com.br/quem-somos)

## Urca Angels

- **Type:** Angel-investment network
- **Website:** https://www.urcaangels.com/
- **Geography:** Brazil
- **Stage:** Pre-seed and Seed
- **Disclosed investment size:** R$300,000 to R$3 million per investment
- **Founder route:** Use the pitch section on the official website or email
  `contact@urcaangels.com`

Urca Angels is a Brazilian network formed primarily by Instituto Militar de
Engenharia alumni. Members invest individually in early-stage technology
startups. The network explicitly states that it is not a venture fund.

### Sources

- [Urca Angels overview, criteria, portfolio, and pitch process](https://www.urcaangels.com/)

**Last verified:** 2026-07-26
