# Innovation hubs and incubators

This category covers active hubs, technology parks and incubators that accept
startups from Latin America and provide structured access to business support,
corporate connections, investors or incubation infrastructure.

These organizations are not presented as direct investors unless a separate,
qualifying investment vehicle is documented in the fund catalog. Individual
programs operated by a hub remain separate accelerator or public-program
profiles when their rules and application process are distinct.

The catalog contains 4 profiles, all verified on 2026-08-04.
