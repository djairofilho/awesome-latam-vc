# AngelHub

- **Type:** Angel-investment club
- **Operator:** AngelHub
- **Website:** https://www.angelhub.mx/
- **Geography:** Mexico and Latin America
- **Stage:** Pre-seed and Seed
- **Disclosed investment size:** US$25,000 to US$250,000, funded by one or more
  members
- **Founder route:** Apply through the official website
- **Recent activity:** Recruiting startups for its next batch in July 2026

AngelHub's team receives and filters applications, interviews selected
startups, and presents them at an Investment Day. Individual club members
decide whether to make offers; one or more members supply the capital. The
official process then provides for due diligence and legal review. The
organization belongs in this index because the disclosed operating model is an
angel club, not a manager making recurring investments from a pooled fund.

No standard instrument or investment terms are stated on the cited pages.
