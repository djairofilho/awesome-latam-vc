# AngelHub

- **Type:** Angel-investment club
- **Website:** https://www.angelhub.mx/
- **Geography:** Mexico and Latin America
- **Stage:** Pre-seed and Seed
- **Disclosed investment size:** US$25,000 to US$250,000, funded by one or more
  members
- **Founder route:** https://www.angelhub.ventures/

AngelHub selects startups and presents them to members of its angel-investment
club. Individual members decide whether to make offers and can invest together.
The organization is listed here rather than in the main fund index because its
official operating model is an angel club, not a recurring venture capital
fund.
