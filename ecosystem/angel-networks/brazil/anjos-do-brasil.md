# Anjos do Brasil

- **Type:** Nonprofit angel-investment ecosystem organization and network
- **Website:** https://anjosdobrasil.net/
- **Geography:** Brazil
- **Founder route:** https://anjosdobrasil.net/submeter-startup/

Anjos do Brasil connects entrepreneurs with angel investors and provides
education, mentoring, events, and ecosystem advocacy. Its official materials
state that the organization does not invest directly or manage third-party
funds; investment decisions and negotiations are made by investors in the
network.
