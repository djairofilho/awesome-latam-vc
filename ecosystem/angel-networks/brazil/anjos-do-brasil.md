# Anjos do Brasil

- **Type:** Nonprofit angel-investment ecosystem organization and network
- **Operator:** Anjos do Brasil
- **Website:** https://anjosdobrasil.net/
- **Geography:** Brazil
- **Founder route:** https://anjosdobrasil.net/submeter-startup/
- **Recent activity:** Published its 2026 annual angel-investment research in
  June 2026

Anjos do Brasil connects entrepreneurs with angel investors and also provides
education, mentoring, events, and ecosystem advocacy. Its team receives and
selects submitted startups before presenting them to investors in the network.
Those investors make the investment decisions, negotiate, and supply the
capital individually.

The organization states that it does not invest directly or manage third-party
funds. The cited materials do not publish a standard check, instrument, or set
of terms for the network.
