# Gávea Angels

Gávea Angels is a Brazilian angel investor association. The network screens
startups and presents selected opportunities to members, who make individual
investment decisions and deploy their own capital.

## Network profile

- **Website:** https://gaveaangels.org/
- **Stage:** Angel, pre-seed and seed
- **Geography:** Brazil
- **Submit a startup:** https://gaveaangels.org/
- **Capital source:** Individual members
