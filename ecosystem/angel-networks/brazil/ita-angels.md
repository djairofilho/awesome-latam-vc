# ITA Angels

- **Type:** Technology-focused angel-investment pool
- **Operator:** Bossanova Investimentos
- **Website:** Not publicly disclosed in the reviewed sources
- **Geography:** Brazil
- **Stage:** Angel and Pre-seed
- **Founder route:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Recent activity:** Participated in the Passabot round announced in January 2026

ITA Angels is an angel-investment pool linked to the ITA community. The
published thesis targets scalable technology companies in software, apps, IoT,
healthtech, fintech, agtech, and energytech. It prefers B2B or B2B2C SaaS and
cloud businesses with a validated MVP, traction, recurring revenue, and more
than one year of formal operation.

The available thesis says Bossanova Investimentos operates the pool. Recent
reporting confirms investment activity under the ITA Angels name, but the
reviewed sources do not disclose a dedicated official website, a founder
application route, a standard check, or universal investment terms.
