# Insper Angels

- **Type:** Alumni angel-investment network
- **Operator:** Insper Angels, an association formed and managed by Insper students and alumni
- **Website:** https://www.insperangels.com.br/
- **Geography:** Brazil
- **Stage:** Angel
- **Founder route:** https://airtable.com/apphgmjftgDDAJdcX/pagAuMKJXHBD2NuiI/form
- **Recent activity:** Participated in the Passabot round announced in January 2026

Insper Angels connects startups with investors from the Insper community. Its
deal-flow team screens applications before selected companies present to
members. Interested investors then evaluate each opportunity, decide
independently whether to invest, and supply the capital.

The official application accepts companies from any sector that already show
revenue or evidence of business-model validation and have high scalability
potential. The reviewed material does not disclose a standard check,
instrument, or set of investment terms.
