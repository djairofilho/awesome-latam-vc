# FEA Angels

FEA Angels is an angel network formed around the FEA-USP community. Startups
can apply for evaluation and potential presentation to members, who make their
own investment decisions.

## Network profile

- **Website:** https://feaangels.com.br/
- **Stage:** Angel, pre-seed and seed
- **Geography:** Brazil
- **Submit a startup:** https://feaangels.com.br/
- **Capital source:** Individual members
