# GVAngels

- **Type:** Alumni angel-investment network
- **Operator:** GVAngels, a nonprofit FGV alumni association
- **Website:** https://gvangels.com.br/
- **Geography:** Brazil
- **Founder route:** https://app.dealum.com/#/company/application/new/53851/usveyzos5n2gm9hdm324ngpeix7uu9g7
- **Recent activity:** Published an updated account of its investment process in
  July 2026

Founded in 2017 by FGV alumni, GVAngels describes itself as an early-stage angel
investment network and explicitly says it is not a FIP. Its team and selection
committees screen external applications for monthly investment forums.
Associates vote on whether a startup advances to due diligence. Interested
members then decide their commitments, sign the documents, and supply the
capital individually.

The official material says most invested companies are in Pre-seed, Angel, or
Seed rounds. It does not disclose a standard check, instrument, or set of terms.
