# Rockstart LATAM

Rockstart LATAM is a Bogotá-based accelerator and early-stage investor
supporting technology startups across Latin America.

- **Operator:** Rockstart
- **Program type:** Accelerator with direct investment

- **Instrument:** Not normalized from the reviewed public materials

- **Geography:** Latin America

## Eligibility and support

The program primarily seeks startups with an MVP, users or customers, and
early traction. Support combines mentoring, operational work, fundraising,
corporate connections, and possible follow-on co-investment through Series B.
