# Apps.co

Apps.co (APPS.CO) is Colombia's public digital entrepreneurship program. It supports the
creation, validation, and growth of technology businesses through structured
training, mentoring, and periodic calls.

## Program profile

- **Website:** https://www.apps.co/
- **Operator:** Ministerio de Tecnologías de la Información y las Comunicaciones; executed by iNNpulsa Colombia
- **Program type:** Digital entrepreneurship support and acceleration
- **Open to external founders:** Yes, subject to each call's requirements
- **Activity status:** Active
- **Application status:** Varies by call
- **Program format:** Courses, workshops, mentoring, product validation, growth support, and immersion
- **Duration:** Varies by track
- **Stage:** Ideation through growth
- **Capital offered:** None; the official program states that it does not provide seed capital
- **Instrument:** Non-financial support
- **Equity:** Not applicable
- **Geography:** Colombia
- **Apply:** https://www.apps.co/
- **Investment vehicle:** No qualifying investment vehicle identified

## Eligibility and application

The program serves Colombian digital entrepreneurs and businesses. Its tracks
have included product development, business-model validation, technology-led
growth, and immersion with customers, allies, investors, and experts.

## Activity signals

The official program overview and opportunities portal confirmed an active
public founder route when reviewed. Enrollment dates and available tracks
remain specific to each published call.
