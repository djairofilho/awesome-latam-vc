# Santander X

Santander X runs recurring local and global awards and challenges for startups
and scaleups. Its Latin American coverage includes Argentina, Brazil, Chile,
Mexico, and Uruguay.

- **Open to external founders:** Yes, subject to country and call requirements

- **Application status:** Periodic calls
- **Program format:** Challenges, pitch competitions, prizes, visibility, and community access
- **Duration:** Varies by call
- **Stage:** Startup and scaleup
- **Capital offered:** Cash prizes vary by local award or global challenge

Santander X 100 community, visibility, training, and connections. The program's
official calendar should be checked before presenting any challenge as open.
