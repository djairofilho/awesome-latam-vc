## Program profile

NVIDIA Inception provides technical resources, preferred pricing, partner
offers, training and connections to the NVIDIA VC Alliance.

## Eligibility and application

Incorporated technology startups under 10 years old can apply regardless of
funding stage. The program has no application fees, deadlines or cohorts.
