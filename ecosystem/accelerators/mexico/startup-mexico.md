# Startup México

Startup México, also known as SUM, is an entrepreneurship platform offering acceleration,
mentoring, innovation programs, and investor connections in Mexico and Latin
America.

## Program profile

- **Website:** https://startupmexico.com/
- **Operator:** Startup México
- **Program type:** Incubation, acceleration, soft landing, and innovation programs
- **Open to external founders:** Yes, subject to each opportunity's requirements
- **Activity status:** Active
- **Application status:** Varies by opportunity
- **Program format:** Mentoring, commercial strategy, digital transformation, pitch preparation, and ecosystem connections
- **Duration:** Varies; the published acceleration service describes a three-month program
- **Stage:** Multi-stage
- **Capital offered:** Not publicly disclosed as a standard program benefit
- **Instrument:** Non-financial support; call-specific benefits may vary
- **Equity:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Geography:** Mexico and Latin America
- **Apply:** https://startupmexico.com/convocatorias/
- **Investment vehicle:** No qualifying investment vehicle identified

## Eligibility and application

Startup México publishes multiple opportunities rather than one permanent
cohort. Founders should verify the eligibility, cost, benefits, and dates of
each current call before applying.

## Activity signals

The official platform, acceleration service, and opportunities page were
active when reviewed. Individual calls may open or close independently.
