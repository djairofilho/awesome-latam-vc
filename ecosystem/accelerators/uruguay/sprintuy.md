# SprintUy

SprintUy is a soft-landing program for international technology startups
considering Uruguay as a base from which to expand across Latin America.

## Program profile

- **Website:** https://anii.org.uy/apoyos/emprendimientos/655/sprintuy-2026/
- **Operator:** Agencia Nacional de Investigación e Innovación (ANII) and Uruguay XXI
- **Program type:** International soft-landing and ecosystem immersion
- **Open to external founders:** Yes; the 2026 call targeted companies incorporated outside Uruguay
- **Activity status:** Active recurring initiative
- **Application status:** Closed for the 2026 edition
- **Program format:** One-week in-country immersion, workshops, networking, and ecosystem connections
- **Duration:** One week
- **Stage:** Early stage with a functional prototype or MVP and demonstrated traction
- **Capital offered:** No cash investment; travel and accommodation for one founder were covered in 2026
- **Instrument:** In-kind travel, accommodation, and program support
- **Equity:** Not applicable
- **Geography:** Uruguay as a base for Latin American expansion
- **Apply:** https://anii.org.uy/apoyos/emprendimientos/655/sprintuy-2026/
- **Investment vehicle:** No qualifying investment vehicle identified

## Eligibility and application

The 2026 edition accepted foreign companies up to five years old whose founder
teams retained at least 51% ownership. Applicants needed a technology or
science-based product, early validation, and a differentiated value proposition.

## Activity signals

ANII published the 2026 edition with official requirements, dates, and program
benefits. Applications for that edition are closed, so it is not presented as
an open call.
