# TD Impacta

TD Impacta is a national support and funding platform created by Tesouro Direto
and operated by Artemisia. The current edition accepts Brazilian impact
businesses focused on financial education and inclusion; support may use grants
or service contracts rather than a standard venture investment.

## Program profile

- **Website:** https://tdimpacta.com.br/
- **Operator:** Artemisia
- **Creator:** Tesouro Direto
- **Program type:** Non-dilutive impact support program
- **Open to external founders:** Yes, subject to the published eligibility rules
- **Activity status:** Active
- **Application status:** Open on the verified date
- **Program format:** National call, selection and implementation support
- **Duration:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Stage:** Validated solutions with implementation capacity
- **Capital offered:** Grants or service contracts under the published call
- **Instrument:** Grant or service contract
- **Equity:** No equity requirement disclosed
- **Geography:** Brazil
- **Apply:** https://tdimpacta.com.br/

## Eligibility and application

The current call accepts Brazilian impact businesses with validated solutions
and implementation capacity in financial education and inclusion. Applicants
must use the official program route and follow the edition-specific rules
and deadlines published there.

## Activity signals

The verified edition is active and accepts applications. Its support can be
structured as grants or service contracts, so the program is not represented
as a conventional equity accelerator or venture fund.
