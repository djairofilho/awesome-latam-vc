# AUGE-UCR

AUGE-UCR is the Universidad de Costa Rica's entrepreneurship agency,
providing incubation and early-stage support to innovative ventures.

## Program profile

- **Website:** https://diprovid.ucr.ac.cr/auge/
- **Operator:** Universidad de Costa Rica
- **Program type:** Pre-incubation and incubation platform
- **Open to external founders:** Yes
- **Activity status:** Active
- **Application status:** Rolling for StartupGo
- **Program format:** Training, coaching, validation, and incubation
- **Duration:** Varies by phase
- **Stage:** Idea validation through initial market traction
- **Capital offered:** Conditional access to non-repayable seed resources
- **Instrument:** Grants administered under applicable program rules
- **Equity:** Not publicly disclosed
- **Geography:** Costa Rica
- **Apply:** https://diprovid.ucr.ac.cr/auge/
- **Investment vehicle:** No separate venture capital vehicle identified

## Eligibility and support

Applicants pass an admission review. Participation can lead to eligibility for
prototype or launch funding, but completing a program does not guarantee a
grant and additional evaluation requirements apply.
