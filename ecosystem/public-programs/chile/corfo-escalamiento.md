# Escalamiento

Escalamiento is a Corfo co-financing program for innovative Chilean companies
that have validated their business model and seek rapid growth, private
investment, or international expansion.

## Program profile

- **Entity ID:** `program-corfo-escalamiento`
- **Entity type:** Public funding program
- **Operator:** [Corporación de Fomento de la Producción](corfo.md) (`agency-corfo`)
- **Official page:** https://www.corfo.gob.cl/sites/cpp/nacional/corfo-abre-convocatoria-de-su-programa-escalamiento-para-emprendimientos-con-alto-impacto-en-chile-y-el-mundo/
- **Geography:** Chile
- **Financial support:** Non-repayable co-financing
- **Program status:** Currently closed; 2026 activity confirmed
- **Founder route:** https://www.corfo.gob.cl/sites/cpp/nacional/corfo-abre-convocatoria-de-su-programa-escalamiento-para-emprendimientos-con-alto-impacto-en-chile-y-el-mundo/

## 2026 call snapshot

The 2026 call closed on July 20. It targeted companies incorporated in Chile,
operating for less than six years, with CLP 80 million to CLP 800 million in
sales during the previous 12 months. Corfo offered up to 70% co-financing,
capped at CLP 60 million, or up to 80% and CLP 68.5 million for women-led
companies.

This is a historical snapshot, not a permanent availability claim. Check the
official route for the next call and its specific terms.
