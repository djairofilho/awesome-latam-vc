# iNNpulsa Colombia

iNNpulsa Colombia is a national public entrepreneurship platform operated
under the Ministerio de Comercio, Industria y Turismo. It connects businesses
and founders with programs, calls, financing routes, and support resources.

## Program profile

- **Website:** https://www.innpulsacolombia.com/
- **Operator:** Ministerio de Comercio, Industria y Turismo
- **Program type:** National entrepreneurship and innovation platform
- **Open to external founders:** Yes, subject to each call's requirements
- **Activity status:** Active
- **Application status:** Unified registration available
- **Program format:** Portfolio of calls, training, and support programs
- **Duration:** Varies by call
- **Stage:** Varies by call
- **Capital offered:** Varies by call
- **Instrument:** Varies by call
- **Equity:** Varies by call
- **Geography:** Colombia
- **Apply:** https://registrounico.innpulsacolombia.com/register
- **Investment vehicle:** No single qualifying investment vehicle identified

## Access route

The unified registration centralizes founder and company information for
access to current iNNpulsa Colombia calls and programs. Eligibility, benefits,
deadlines, and financial terms remain specific to each published call.
