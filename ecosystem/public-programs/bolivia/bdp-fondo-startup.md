# Fondo de Capital de Riesgo para Emprendimientos Innovadores

- **Entity ID:** `program-bdp-fondo-startup`
- **Entity type:** Public funding program
- **Operator:** [Banco de Desarrollo Productivo](bdp-bolivia.md) (`agency-bdp-bolivia`)
- **Aliases:** Fondo Startup, BDP Fondo Startup
- **Official page:** https://www.bdp.com.bo/fondo-startup/
- **Geography:** Bolívia
- **Financial support:** capital, credit
- **Program status:** Active
- **Activity basis:** permanent intake observed at the cutoff
- **Founder route:** https://www.bdp.com.bo/fondo-startup/
- **Publication batch:** `public-programs-02`

A página vigente oferece capital de risco direto ou indireto a startups bolivianas inovadoras ligadas a exportações ou substituição de importações, informa condições financeiras e mantém documentos do fundo referentes a 2025.

Availability is based on the official route observed at the cutoff. Terms can change, so founders must confirm the current instrument.

## Agency relationship

- Operated by [Banco de Desarrollo Productivo](bdp-bolivia.md).

## Calls

No temporary call is attached to the frozen eligible record. Use the official founder route for the current intake.
