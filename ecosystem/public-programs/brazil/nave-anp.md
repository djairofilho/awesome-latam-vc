# NAVE ANP

NAVE ANP is an open-innovation program connecting Brazilian startups with
energy companies around shared technology challenges. It is explicitly not an
accelerator; participating companies fund selected research and development
projects under the program rules.

## Program profile

- **Official page:** https://www.gov.br/anp/pt-br/assuntos/tecnologia-meio-ambiente/nave-1
- **Operator:** Agência Nacional do Petróleo, Gás Natural e Biocombustíveis
- **Geography:** Brazil
- **Program status:** Current selected projects in execution
- **Founder route:** https://www.gov.br/anp/pt-br/assuntos/tecnologia-meio-ambiente/nave-1
