# Pesquisa Inovativa em Pequenas Empresas

- **Entity ID:** `program-fapesp-pipe`
- **Entity type:** Public funding program
- **Operator:** [Fundação de Amparo à Pesquisa do Estado de São Paulo](fapesp.md) (`agency-fapesp`)
- **Aliases:** PIPE
- **Official page:** https://fapesp.br/pipe/
- **Geography:** São Paulo, Brasil
- **Financial support:** grant or non-repayable funding
- **Program status:** Active
- **Activity basis:** permanent intake observed at the cutoff
- **Founder route:** https://fapesp.br/pipe/faq
- **Publication batch:** `public-programs-03`

A FAPESP informa que recebe propostas PIPE Fase 1, Fase 2 e PIPE Invest em fluxo contínuo ao longo do ano e divulga limites de até R$ 300 mil e R$ 1,5 milhão conforme a fase.

Availability is based on the official route observed at the cutoff. Terms can change, so founders must confirm the current instrument.

- Operated by [Fundação de Amparo à Pesquisa do Estado de São Paulo](fapesp.md).

- **Call ID:** `call-fapesp-pipe-jornada-2026-1`
- **Status:** Open at the cutoff snapshot
- **Snapshot date:** 2026-07-27
- **Opened on:** 2026-06-29
- **Closed or closes on:** 2026-07-29
- **Call route:** https://fapesp.br/pipe/jornada-tecnologica-2026-fase-1
